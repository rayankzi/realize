# The 5 LinkedIn Post Types That Convert Followers Into Customers

**Topic/Category:** LinkedIn Marketing / Content Strategy
**Primary Goal of the Post:** Teaches the five specific LinkedIn post formats — mapped to a marketing funnel — that turn a LinkedIn profile into a lead generation machine, driving awareness, trust, and conversions.

---

## Executive Summary

This carousel by Chris Donnelly breaks down a complete LinkedIn content funnel, mapping five distinct post types to the top, middle, and bottom of a marketing funnel. The core thesis is that most people treat LinkedIn like a CV when it should function as an always-on landing page. By strategically rotating through Awareness Posts, Personal Story Posts, Education Posts, "How We Do It" Posts, and Conversion Posts, creators can move their audience from discovery to purchase. Donnelly credits this framework with growing his following from 20K to 1.2M and generating millions in sales over four years.

## Key Insights & Main Ideas

- **LinkedIn is a funnel, not a resume:** The five post types map directly to Top of Funnel (awareness), Middle of Funnel (trust & education), and Bottom of Funnel (conversion).
- **Each post type has a specific job:** Awareness posts get reach, Personal Stories build trust, Education posts establish authority, "How We Do It" posts differentiate you, and Conversion posts close deals.
- **Pre-validated content reduces risk:** Awareness posts should leverage quotes, billboards, or viral topics — ideas already proven to resonate — to gain traction.
- **Personal stories follow a narrative formula:** They use a Relatable Pain Point hook, a Body (transition, context, story), a Scene section (transparency, personality), and end with either a Win (credibility) or a Loss (honesty/empathy).
- **Education posts double as lead magnets:** Infographics and cheat sheets push audiences toward newsletters and other owned channels.
- **IP and frameworks are your moat:** The "How We Do It" post positions proprietary methodology (like the "PLAY" Framework shown) as a differentiator that builds authority and justifies premium pricing.
- **Conversion posts need an MVP (Most Valuable Proof):** A case study featuring a customer with the same struggle, showing the transformation, and highlighting tangible, measurable results.

## Detailed Breakdown / Step-by-Step

**Slide 1 — Title Card:** "The 5 Best LinkedIn Posts To Get Customers In 2026" — sets the hook and promise.

**Slide 2 — Top of Funnel: The Awareness Post**
- Purpose: Traction and attention using pre-validated ideas.
- Aligns ideas and values with your personal brand.
- Format communicates simple ideas in an easy-to-read, engaging way.
- Use: Quotes, Billboards, or Viral topics to gain awareness.
- Example hook shown: "If you can spend 8 hours working on someone else's dream... You can spend 60 minutes working on your own."

**Slide 3 — Middle of Funnel: The Personal Story Post**
- Purpose: Build deeper relationships, trust, and empathy.
- Structure provided:
  - **Relatable Pain Point:** Contrast Hook (conflicting statement vs. expected reality), Simple language, Relatable scenario.
  - **Body:** Transition (smooth flow), Context (background behind the story), Further Context (more information), Transparency (radical transparency that builds trust), Personality (goals reveal character).
  - **Win:** Rise (start with a win or achievement), Credibility (achievements build authority).
  - **Losses:** Honesty (setbacks build connection), Agitate (detail how damaging something is to be raw).

**Slide 4 — Middle of Funnel: The Education Post**
- Purpose: Educate and stimulate your audience on your content pillars.
- Educates on key areas of their business.
- Positions you as a trustworthy resource for high-quality info and problem-solving.
- Gives actionable step-by-step information.
- Works best as a carousel or infographic for visual engagement.
- Example shown: "The Business Strategy Cheat Sheet" — pushes toward a newsletter, acting as a lead magnet.

**Slide 5 — Middle of Funnel: The "How We Do It" Post**
- Purpose: Educate audience on your methodology, IP, and what you do.
- Solves their problem while differentiating with your own data and frameworks.
- Key reasons: IP sets you apart in crowded markets, people pay for differentiation (unique proven approaches), and sharing your approach builds thought leadership and authority.
- Example shown: The "PLAY" Framework — a proprietary visual framework demonstrating methodology.

**Slide 6 — Bottom of Funnel: The Conversion Post**
- Purpose: Show how your methodology has previously delivered the exact results your customer wants.
- MVP (Most Valuable Proof) should include:
  1. A customer who had the same struggle as your target audience.
  2. How your methodology/framework/system helped them achieve a transformation.
  3. Tangible, measurable results (e.g., revenue growth, time saved, conversions increased).
- Includes a Conversion Funnel diagram: Pre-Validated Content (reach & following) → Consideration Content (trust & authority) → Conversion Content (followers into customers). Not everyone is ready to buy on day one.

**Slide 7 — CTA: "The LinkedIn Conversion Playbook"**

- Tagline: "Stop Treating LinkedIn Like a CV..."
- Positions the playbook as the tool to turn your profile into an always-on lead gen machine.
