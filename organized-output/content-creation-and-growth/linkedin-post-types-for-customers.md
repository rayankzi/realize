# The 5 Best LinkedIn Posts to Get Customers in 2026

**Topic/Category:** LinkedIn Marketing / Content Strategy
**Primary Goal of the Post:** To teach entrepreneurs and business owners the five specific post types that drive customer acquisition on LinkedIn, mapped to a top-to-bottom conversion funnel.

---

## Executive Summary

This carousel breaks down a proven LinkedIn content strategy built around five distinct post formats, each designed for a specific stage of the customer conversion funnel. The creator, who grew from 20K to 1.2M followers and generated millions in sales over four years, reveals that most people fail on LinkedIn not because the platform is dead, but because they're posting the wrong types of content. The framework maps Awareness, Personal Story, Education, How We Do It, and Conversion posts across top-of-funnel, middle-of-funnel, and bottom-of-funnel stages to systematically turn a LinkedIn profile into an always-on lead generation machine.

## Key Insights & Main Ideas

- **LinkedIn is a funnel, not a feed:** Not everyone is ready to buy on day one. Content must be strategically mapped across awareness, consideration, and conversion stages.
- **Only 5 post types are needed:** Rather than posting randomly, focusing on these five formats creates a repeatable system that drives follower growth and sales.
- **Awareness posts use pre-validated ideas:** Quotes, billboards, and viral topics are used to gain traction and align your personal brand with certain values — top of funnel.
- **Personal stories build trust through vulnerability:** Using a structured format (Relatable Pain Point > Break > Story > Win/Lesson), these posts nurture deeper audience relationships — middle of funnel.
- **Education posts position you as a trusted resource:** Carousels and infographics that teach actionable information also double as lead magnets pushing toward newsletters — middle of funnel.
- **"How We Do It" posts differentiate through IP:** Sharing your unique frameworks, processes, and methodologies establishes thought leadership and justifies premium pricing — middle of funnel.
- **Conversion posts use social proof (MVPs):** Showcasing real customer transformations with tangible, measurable results converts followers into customers — bottom of funnel.

## Detailed Breakdown / Step-by-Step

**Slide 1 — Cover:** "The 5 Best LinkedIn Posts To Get Customers In 2026" — sets up the promise of the carousel.

**Slide 2 — Top of Funnel: The Awareness Post:**
- Designed to get traction and attention using pre-validated ideas and concepts.
- Aligns certain ideas and values with your personal brand.
- Communicates simple ideas in an easy-to-read, engaging format.
- Best use cases: Quotes, Billboards, or Viral topics.
- Example shown: "If you can spend 8 hours working on someone else's dream... You can spend 60 minutes working on your own."

**Slide 3 — Middle of Funnel: The Personal Story Post:**
- Builds deeper relationships, connection, trust, and empathy.
- Structured in four parts:
  - **Relatable Pain Point:** Contrast Hook (conflicting statement vs. expected reality), Simple language, Relatable scenario.
  - **Break:** Transition (allow the story to flow smoothly), Context (provide context behind the story).
  - **Story:** Further Context, Transparency (radical transparency which builds trust), Personality (your goals say a lot about who you are).
  - **Win or Lesson:** Rise (best stories start with a win or achievement), Credibility (achievements build authority) OR Honesty (setbacks build connection), Agitate (explain in detail how damaging a situation was).

**Slide 4 — Middle of Funnel: The Education Post:**
- Educates and stimulates your audience on your content pillars.
- Focuses on educating on key areas of business.
- Positions you as a trustworthy resource for high-quality info and problem-solving.
- Gives actionable step-by-step information.
- Works best as a carousel or infographic for visual hooks and complex data.
- The post pushes toward a newsletter, acting as a lead magnet.

**Slide 5 — Middle of Funnel: The How We Do It Post:**
- Educates your audience on your methodology, IP, and what you do.
- Aims to solve the audience's problem and differentiate you with proprietary data and frameworks.
- Three key reasons to use this format:
  - **IP sets you apart:** Frameworks, processes, and methodologies become defensible assets that establish credibility.
  - **People pay for differentiation:** Customers want a unique, proven way to solve their challenges.
  - **Trust builds authority:** Sharing your approach positions you as a thought leader, not just another service provider.
- Example shown: The "PLAY" Framework visual.

**Slide 6 — Bottom of Funnel: The Conversion Post:**
- Shows how your methodology has previously delivered exact results your customer wants.
- MVP (Most Valuable Proof) structure:
  1. A customer who had the same struggle as your target audience.
  2. How your methodology, framework, or system helped them achieve a transformation.
  3. Tangible, measurable results (e.g., revenue growth, time saved, conversions increased).
- Includes a visual conversion funnel diagram:
  - **Pre-Validated Content** (top) — drives following, reach, and engagement.
  - **Consideration Content** (middle) — boosts trust and authority.
  - **Conversion Content** (bottom) — converts followers into customers.
