# Best Remote Job Boards to Actually Find Legit Remote Work

**Topic/Category:** Career / Remote Work / Job Search
**Primary Goal of the Post:** To share five curated, remote-specific job boards that filter out fake or hybrid-labeled "remote" listings, helping job seekers find legitimate fully-remote positions.

---

## Executive Summary

With only 8% of job listings being fully remote yet attracting 40%+ of all applications, the remote job market is intensely competitive — and cluttered with misleading hybrid postings disguised as remote roles. This carousel argues that mainstream job boards like Indeed are inadequate for remote job seekers and instead recommends five niche, remote-specific platforms (No Desk, Flexa, Remote 100K, Built In, and Himalayas) that do a far better job filtering for genuinely remote opportunities. All five are free to browse, though the creator advises against using their paid AI auto-application features.

## Key Insights & Main Ideas

- **Only 8% of current listings are fully remote**, yet they receive over 40% of all job applications — making remote roles extremely competitive.
- **Some companies falsely advertise hybrid roles as "remote"** to attract more applicants, making general job boards unreliable for remote-specific searches.
- **Remote-specific job boards do a much better job filtering** for truly remote positions compared to mainstream platforms like Indeed.
- **All five recommended platforms are free to browse** — paid AI auto-application features exist but are not recommended by the creator.
- **Different boards serve different experience levels and geographies** — matching the right board to your profile matters.

- **Slide 1 (Cover):** Hook — "Finding remote jobs is F*CKED (Stop using Indeed & use these instead)"

- **Slide 8 — Himalayas:** Tech-focused board listing opportunities with smaller startups. These roles are nearly always remote because the startups can't yet afford office space.
