## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **WHO > WHAT:** The person you reach out to matters far more than the message itself. Spend time finding the right person before crafting your outreach.
- **Multi-filter LinkedIn strategy:** Stack filters (school, company, location, role, interests) to find high-overlap contacts who are more likely to respond and connect meaningfully.
- **Coffee chats can be informal interviews:** In industries like investment banking and consulting, your coffee chat performance directly influences whether you get an interview. Treat them accordingly.
- **Ask un-Googleable questions:** Focus on personal perspective questions that demonstrate genuine curiosity and research, not surface-level queries.
- **Use AI for prep:** Paste the person's LinkedIn profile and your resume into an LLM to identify mutual interest areas and generate targeted conversation topics.
- **Track everything:** Use a Notion spreadsheet (or similar tool) to log dates, discussion notes, and follow-up reminders. What you're not tracking, you're losing.
- **Always follow through:** Send thank-you messages, set calendar reminders for follow-ups, and actually keep promises to "stay in touch."

## Narrative & Step-by-Step Breakdown

**Step 1: Filtered Outreach on LinkedIn**
1. Identify your target companies, roles, and industries.
2. Use LinkedIn's advanced search filters: Your School + Current Company + Location + Desired Company + "Desired Role" + shared interests.
3. Look for people who check multiple boxes (same school, similar interests, desired career path) to maximize connection potential.
4. The creator personally filters for niche interests like theater, creator economy, and a cappella to find unique common ground.
5. Rule of thumb: use email for consulting outreach, LinkedIn DMs for tech outreach.

**Step 2: Coffee Chat Preparation**
1. Research the person's industry and career trajectory thoroughly before the chat.
2. Memorize their career path — don't ask things you could easily find on their profile.
3. Prepare personal perspective questions like: "How did you navigate X?" or "I noticed you did X, what was your decision criteria to make Y pivot?"
4. Advanced move: paste their LinkedIn URL and your resume into ChatGPT or another LLM, then ask it to generate mutual interest topics and conversation starters.

**Step 3: Network Tracker + Reminder System**
1. Maintain a Notion spreadsheet (or equivalent) with columns for: dates, discussion topics, follow-up dates, and notes.
2. Send a thank-you message immediately after every coffee chat.
3. Set Google Calendar reminders to follow up in X months.
4. If you promised to "keep them updated," actually do it — follow-through builds trust and long-term relationships.
