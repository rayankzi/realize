# The LinkedIn Networking Playbook: Filtered Outreach, Coffee Chats & Follow-Ups

**Topic/Category:** Career Networking / Professional Development
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** Teach viewers a structured, three-step system for making high-impact professional connections through LinkedIn outreach, coffee chat preparation, and ongoing relationship tracking.

## Executive Summary

This reel lays out a complete networking framework aimed at early-career professionals and job seekers. The creator breaks the process into three phases: (1) using LinkedIn's advanced filters to find the *right* people to reach out to, (2) preparing for coffee chats with the rigor of an informal interview, and (3) maintaining a tracking system so connections don't go cold. The overarching message is that networking should be strategic, well-prepared, and relationship-oriented rather than transactional.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Insight 1:** WHO you talk to matters more than WHAT you say. Spending effort to find the right person through filtered outreach yields far better results than mass messaging.
- **Insight 2:** Coffee chats in industries like investment banking and consulting are effectively informal interviews — your "performance" can influence who gets a formal interview.
- **Insight 3:** Use an LLM (ChatGPT) to cross-reference a contact's LinkedIn profile with your resume to surface mutual interests and generate tailored conversation topics.
- **Insight 4:** Tracking every coffee chat in a structured system (like a Notion spreadsheet) with follow-up dates and notes is essential — what you're not tracking, you're losing.
- **Insight 5:** Networking is a long game. The goal is building win-win relationships, not one-time transactional exchanges.

## Narrative & Step-by-Step Breakdown

**Phase 1 — Filtered Outreach on LinkedIn**
1. Prioritize reaching out to people who check multiple boxes: same school, desired career path, shared interests.
2. Apply LinkedIn filters strategically: Your School + Current Company + Location + Desired Company + "Desired Role" + "Interests."
3. The creator personally filters for niche interests like theater, creator economy, and a cappella to find strong connection points.
4. Rule of thumb: use email for Consulting outreach, LinkedIn DMs for Tech.

**Phase 2 — Coffee Chat Preparation**
1. Research the person's industry, shared interests, and memorize their career trajectory before the chat.
2. Ask questions you cannot easily Google — focus on personal perspective questions like "How did you navigate X?" or "I noticed you did X, what was your decision criteria to make Y pivot?"
3. Next-level prep: paste the person's LinkedIn URL and your resume into ChatGPT, then ask it to generate mutual-interest topics and conversation questions.

**Phase 3 — Network Tracker & Reminder System**
1. Keep all coffee chat notes in a Notion spreadsheet with columns for dates, discussion topics, and follow-up dates.
2. Always send a thank-you message after every coffee chat.
3. Set Google Calendar reminders to follow up in X months, and if you promised to "keep them updated," actually follow through.
