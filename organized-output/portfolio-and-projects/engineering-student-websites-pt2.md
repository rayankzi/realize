# Essential Websites Every Engineering Student Must Know (Part 2)

**Topic/Category:** Engineering / Education / Student Resources
**Primary Goal of the Post:** To share a curated list of websites that help engineering students find contests, project inspiration, tutorials, and community — all valuable for building portfolios and gaining hands-on experience.

## Executive Summary

This carousel (Part 2 of a series) highlights four key online platforms that engineering students should leverage to accelerate their learning and build impressive project portfolios. The creator shares personal experience as a beginner, recommending these sites for finding engineering contests with real prize money, discovering project tutorials at every skill level, and curating inspiration through social media feeds. The overarching message is that these free resources can fast-track an engineering student from newbie to competent builder.

## Key Insights & Main Ideas

- **Hackster.io** is a hub for engineering contests with up to $5,000 in prizes, doubling as a project catalogue and developer community.
- **Instructables** (by Autodesk) offers engineering contests with $500+ prizes and serves as a rich source of project tutorials ranging from beginner to pro level.
- **Adafruit Learn** provides beginner-friendly guides on electronics modules and is an excellent starting point for students new to hardware projects.
- **Instagram itself** can be a powerful learning tool when used intentionally — following inspiring engineering pages curates a feed that keeps you motivated and exposes you to new ideas without having to reinvent the wheel.
