# Email Templates to Send Your Resume to Recruiters

**Topic/Category:** Career / Job Search
**Primary Goal of the Post:** Provide ready-to-use email templates for job seekers to send their resumes to recruiters and hiring managers via email, LinkedIn DM, or cold outreach.

---

## Executive Summary

This carousel compiles six distinct email and message templates designed for different stages and scenarios of the job application process. From sending a resume in response to a job posting, to cold-outreach DMs to hiring managers, to graceful follow-ups after a rejection, the post equips job seekers with professional, fill-in-the-blank scripts they can customize for their own profiles and target roles. The caption reinforces that these templates should be personalized to match each applicant's profile and job description.

## Key Insights & Main Ideas

- **Multiple scenarios covered:** The templates span the full job-search communication lifecycle — initial application, cover letter email, post-rejection follow-up, cold outreach to hiring managers, and proactive job-seeker introductions.
- **Fill-in-the-blank format:** Every template uses bracketed placeholders (e.g., [Name], [Job Name], [Company]) so users can quickly customize them.
- **Professional but approachable tone:** The language across all templates strikes a balance between formality and warmth, suitable for most industries.
- **Cold outreach included:** Two templates specifically address reaching out to recruiters or hiring managers without a specific job posting, which is a less commonly shared but highly effective job-search strategy.
- **Customization is key:** The caption explicitly reminds users to tailor each template to their own profile and the specific job description.

## Verbatim Templates & Scripts

**Template 1: Sample Email to Send Resume to Recruiter**

> Dear [Recruiter Name],
>
> I am [Name] and I am interested in the post of [Job Name]. My resume is attached to this email.
>
> After reading the job description, I believe I possess all the necessary abilities and credentials. Details about my current employment and previous roles are also available.
>
> Can you describe the steps in the process and how they will be completed?
>
> You can reach me at [insert details]. I am interested in your response regarding how to proceed with my application.
>
> Your Name.

**Template 2: Sample Email to Send Resume to Job Role**

> Dear [Recruiter's Name],
>
> My name is [Name] and I am submitting an application for the job of [Job Name]. A copy of my resume is attached to this email.
>
> I have [X] year of industry experience and a lot of transferable knowledge. I have read the job description and I think I am qualified to perform the job well.
>
> I am eager to apply for the position of [Job Name] because it's a fantastic opportunity in a fantastic company.
>
> Please don't hesitate to get in touch with me if you have any questions [Insert contact details]. Will you kindly confirm that you received my email and inform me of the procedure's next step?
>
> Best regards,
> [Your Name]

**Template 3: Sample Email Cover Letter with Attached Resume**

> Dear [Recruiter Name',
>
> I am making an application for the job of [Title Name] at [Company].
>
> Please find my CV and cover letter attached as stated in the job description. I describe my motivation for applying for a job, my prior experience, and my pay goals in my cover letter.
>
> You can reach me at any time at [Insert Phone Number] or by email if you have any questions Insert [Address].
>
> Regards,
> [Your Name.]

**Template 4: Thank You for Considering My Resume Email**

> Dear [Recruiter Name],
>
> Thank you for taking a look at my application for the post of [Position Name] at [Company Name.]
>
> Even though I was unhappy that I wasn't selected for the interview, I can see why. If it's feasible, I'd like to continue in touch with you, so please send me information on any upcoming opportunities you think I could be a good fit for.
>
> I prefer to be reached by email at [Insert details.]
>
> Please feel free to keep my resume on file, and don't be afraid to get in touch with me if you have any inquiries.

**Template 5: Email / DM to Hiring Manager**

> Hi (First Name),
>
> I saw your profile while looking up (Industry Name) recruiters on LinkedIn in (Name of Place).
>
> I am a (Insert Job Title), (Add relevant information about your experience). My main skills encompass (add skills). Does that fit the type of roles you generally recruit for?
>
> I would love to work for (add company name) as their vision and mission are in line with mine. Let me know what you think, I'd love to talk.
>
> Best,
> (Your Name)

**Template 6: As a Job Seeker (General Outreach)**

> Greetings, (First Name)
>
> My name is (Name), and I am a (Insert Role). I have been following your organization's work for a while now.
>
> (Add notable achievements of company) I am very interested in working at (Insert something unique to the company). (Insert some information about your experience)
>
> If you have a second, I would love to discuss how my experience and skills can benefit your organization for the position of (Insert name of role).
>
> (If there is no job posting, and you want to inquire about an opening then add the line mentioned below.
>
> I know you must be flooded with messages; but if there are any job openings for (Insert Role), I would be more than delighted to share why I might be a good fit.
>
> Thank you and have an amazing day
> Best,
> (Your Name)
