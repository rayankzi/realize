# Writing Viral Scripts with Claude: Stop Using AI as a Ghostwriter

**Topic/Category:** Content Creation / AI Tools
**Primary Goal of the Post:** To teach content creators how to properly prompt Claude AI so it produces authentic, on-brand content rather than generic, robotic-sounding output — reframing Claude as a "thinking partner" rather than a ghostwriter.

---

## Executive Summary

Most people use Claude (and AI in general) by simply asking it to "write something" — and end up with content that sounds like a robot wrote it at 2am. This carousel by @arushiiishokeen reframes the entire approach: Claude works best when you treat it as a thinking partner, giving it rich context about your audience, tone, and emotional goals before asking it to create anything. With the right setup prompts, creators can generate a full week of content in 10 minutes using just 3 prompts.

## Key Insights & Main Ideas

- **The Core Mistake:** People ask Claude to write without providing context, resulting in generic, impersonal content.
- **The Reframe:** Claude isn't a ghostwriter — it's a thinking partner. The output quality is directly tied to the context you give it.
- **Context = Authenticity:** When you tell Claude who you're talking to, your real tone (with examples), and the feeling you want to leave readers with, the content sounds like *you*.
- **Speed + Quality:** With the right prompting method, 3 prompts + 10 minutes = a full week of content.
