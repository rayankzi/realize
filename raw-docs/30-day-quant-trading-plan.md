## Narrative & Step-by-Step Breakdown

**Week 1 — Probability Foundations (Days 1–7):**
Focus on conditional probability, expected value, and basic counting. Solve one timed problem per day — out loud, not silently. Goal: build reasoning instinct, not finish a textbook.

**Week 2 — Statistics and Intuition (Days 8–14):**
Cover distributions, variance, covariance, and the central limit theorem — framed as thinking tools for uncertainty, not memorizable definitions. By day 14, you should be able to explain why correlated assets amplify portfolio risk and why quant strategies need large sample sizes to demonstrate genuine edge.

**Week 3 — Python and Building (Days 15–21):**
One small, complete, documented project per day:
- Day 15: Coin flip simulator
- Day 17: Moving average backtest
- Day 19: Monte Carlo simulation
- Day 21: Correlation matrix

**Week 4 — Interview Preparation (Days 22–30):**
Timed mock problems daily. Walk through Python projects out loud as if an interviewer is watching. Identify every gap in reasoning and close it before applications open.
