# The 7-Day Waitlist Growth System: How to Get 30,000+ Signups

**Topic/Category:** Startup Growth, Marketing Strategy, AI/Tech
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To teach creators and founders a systematic approach to validating a product and generating a large waitlist using targeted content and personalized landing pages.

---

## Executive Summary

The video outlines a "secret system" used to acquire over 30,000 waitlist signups for a startup. The core strategy revolves around identifying seven distinct user personas, creating targeted short-form video content for each, and driving traffic to seven unique landing pages tailored specifically to those personas. By testing these different angles over a seven-day period, founders can identify which user segment is most interested in their product and double down on what works.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Platform Selection:** Choose your platform based on your niche: Instagram is better for tech and software, while TikTok excels for consumer apps and physical products.
- **Persona-Driven Content:** Instead of generic marketing, break your product down into seven specific user personas and script a video for each.
- **Winning Formats:** Don't reinvent the wheel; analyze your niche to find "winning formats" (viral structures) and remix them for your product.
- **Hyper-Personalization:** Use tools like Claude Code to quickly generate seven different landing pages, ensuring the headline and benefits match the specific persona targeted in the video.
- **Automated Conversion:** Use DM automation (like ManyChat) triggered by a specific keyword in the comments to send viewers directly to the relevant waitlist page.
- **Rapid Validation:** Posting daily for seven days provides immediate data on which user type is most likely to convert, allowing for rapid iteration and focus.

## Narrative & Step-by-Step Breakdown

1. **Platform Choice:** Identify if your product fits better on Instagram (Tech/SaaS) or TikTok (Consumer).
2. **Identify Personas:** List seven different types of people (personas) who would realistically buy or use your product.
3. **Scripting:** Write seven scripts, each targeting one specific persona. Use proven viral structures from your niche.
4. **CTA & Automation:** Include a Call to Action (CTA) at the end of each video asking for a "trigger word" comment to automate link delivery.
5. **Page Creation:** Build seven distinct waitlist/landing pages tailored to the seven personas (using Claude Code for speed).
6. **Execution:** Post one video per day for seven days straight.
7. **Analysis:** Review the data to see which persona and page performed the best, then focus your efforts there.
