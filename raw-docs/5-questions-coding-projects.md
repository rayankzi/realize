# 5 Essential Questions for Your Next Coding Project

**Topic/Category:** Software Engineering / Project Planning
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To help developers plan their projects more effectively by asking five critical questions regarding goals, scale, bottlenecks, scope, and success metrics.

---

## Executive Summary

This reel addresses common pitfalls in project development, such as over-engineering and lack of clear objectives. It emphasizes aligning your technology choices with your ultimate goal—whether it's commercial success or career advancement—and maintaining a lean, scalable approach to building features.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Goal Alignment:** Choose your tech stack based on your goal. Simple stacks for profit; industry-standard stacks for learning/employment.
- **Realistic Scaling:** Your expected user count should dictate your infrastructure decisions. Start small but have a scaling plan.
- **Anticipate Failures:** Identify potential bottlenecks (database, server, or budget) before they become issues.
- **Aggressive Scoping:** Focus on a few core features rather than building unnecessary complexity that prevents launching.
- **Define Success:** Use measurable outcomes (e.g., income for businesses, interviews/knowledge for learning) to determine if the project is successful.

## Narrative & Step-by-Step Breakdown

The video follows a structured informational format:

1.  **Question 1: What is your goal?**
    *   If money: Use simple tech and cheap hosting (VPS).
    *   If career: Use tools required by employers in your target field.
2.  **Question 2: How many users do you expect?**
    *   This influences scaling, costs, and monitoring.
    *   Advice: Start small but plan for quick growth.
3.  **Question 3: What is the first thing that will break?**
    *   Identify bottlenecks early (DB, server, wallet).
    *   Have a contingency plan.
4.  **Question 4: What can I *not* build yet?**
    *   Avoid feature creep; focus on the 3 essential features instead of 100.
    *   Knowing what to omit is as vital as knowing what to include.
5.  **Question 5: How do you know if it's working?**
    *   Learning goal: Assess knowledge gain and job interview frequency.
    *   Business goal: Assess profitability.
