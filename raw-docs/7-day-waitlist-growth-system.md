# 30K+ Waitlist Signup System: The 7-Day Multi-Persona Framework

**Topic/Category:** Startup Growth / Digital Marketing / Lead Generation
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To share a proven strategy for generating high-volume waitlist signups (30,000+) for a startup by leveraging multi-persona targeting and automated DM funnels.

---

## Executive Summary

The creator outlines a systematic approach to startup growth that moves beyond generic marketing. By breaking down a product's potential audience into seven distinct user personas and creating tailored content and landing pages for each, founders can identify their most profitable niche within a week. The system relies on short-form video (Instagram or TikTok), landing page generation via Claude Code, and DM automation to drive conversions.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Platform-Product Alignment:** Choose your battlefield wisely—Instagram is superior for tech/software, while TikTok excels for consumer-facing apps and physical products.
- **The "Persona x7" Rule:** Don't market to everyone. Identify seven specific types of people who would buy your product and treat them as independent markets.
- **Conversion through Specificity:** High conversion rates come from matching a specific video hook to a specific landing page headline.
- **Iterative Testing:** The 7-day sprint isn't just for growth; it's a data-gathering exercise to see which user type has the highest intent.
- **Automation is Mandatory:** Scaling to 30k+ signups requires a "trigger word" system (like ManyChat or Inro) to handle lead flow instantly.

## Narrative & Step-by-Step Breakdown

1. **Pick the Right Platform:** Use Instagram for tech/B2B/software; use TikTok for consumer/lifestyle/B2C.
2. **Identify 7 User Personas:** Brainstorm seven different types of individuals or professionals who have the problem your product solves.
3. **Script 7 Targeted Videos:** Write one script for each persona. Use "winning formats" already proven in your niche.
4. **Build 7 Custom Landing Pages:** Use Claude Code to rapidly spin up seven distinct waitlist pages, each with a headline and benefits tailored to one of the seven personas.
5. **Automate DMs:** Set up a trigger word (e.g., "PAGE") so that when someone comments, they receive a link to the corresponding landing page automatically.
6. **The 7-Day Sprint:** Post one video per day for seven days.
7. **Analyze & Double Down:** Review the sign-up data to see which persona/page combination performed best and focus future efforts there.
