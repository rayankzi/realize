# 90-Day Roadmap to Securing a Software Engineering Role

**Topic/Category:** Career Development / Software Engineering
**Primary Goal of the Post:** To provide a comprehensive, 90-day strategic roadmap for landing a software engineering job, emphasizing positioning and impact over just coding skills.

---

## Executive Summary

Landing a Software Engineering (SWE) role isn't just about how much you can code; it's about how you position yourself to recruiters and the impact of the projects you build. This guide breaks down a 90-day plan that shifts focus from "spamming LeetCode" to high-signal activities like building niche, user-facing projects, securing referrals through alumni networking, and applying early to maximize visibility.

## Key Insights & Main Ideas

- **Positioning Over Skills:** Recruiters filter by keywords first. You must pick a specific niche (Frontend, Backend, ML) and tailor your resume to match job descriptions exactly to avoid auto-rejection.
- **High-Signal Projects:** One project with real users (20-50) is worth more than five generic "tutorial" projects. Avoid "instant skip" items like to-do apps or calculators.
- **Referrals are Key:** Cold applying is a lottery. Increase your odds by building genuine connections with alumni on LinkedIn before asking for a referral.
- **Impact-Driven Resume:** Shift your language from what tools you used to the impact you created (e.g., "Built X -> used by Y users -> improved Z").
- **Interview Communication:** Technical proficiency is baseline; the differentiator is your ability to explain your thinking patterns out loud.

## Detailed Breakdown / Step-by-Step

- **Day 1-7: Fix Your Positioning**
    - Stop focusing solely on learning new skills.
    - Pick ONE specific role (Backend, ML, or Frontend).
    - Rewrite your resume to match the keywords and requirements of those specific job descriptions.
- **Day 7-30: Build 1-3 High-Signal Projects**
    - Focus on quality over quantity. Aim for one standout project.
    - Requirements: Solves a niche problem, is deployed and usable, and attracts real users (even a small group of 20-50).
    - **Avoid:** To-do apps, calculators, and tutorial clones.
- **Day 15-60: Apply Early**
    - Track job openings daily and apply the moment applications drop.
    - Don't wait for a "perfect" resume; timing is often more important than perfection.
- **Referrals Strategy**
    - Find alumni on LinkedIn.
    - Reach out with a low-friction message: "Quick question about your role."
    - Build a conversation first, then ask for a referral.
- **Day 30-75: Turn Projects into "Experience"**
    - Reframe project descriptions on your resume.
    - Instead of "Built a web app," use: "Built X -> used by Y users -> improved Z."
- **Day 45-90: Prepare for Interviews**
    - Don't just spam random LeetCode problems.
    - Learn core patterns (Arrays, Graphs, DP).
    - Practice explaining your thought process out loud during problem-solving.
