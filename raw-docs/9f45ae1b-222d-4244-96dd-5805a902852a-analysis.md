# Make $30K/Month With Claude: The Digital Product Script Strategy

**Topic/Category:** Online Business / AI Monetization / Digital Products
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To show how the creator uses Claude to build and sell digital products, attracting buyers through targeted content rather than viral reach — and to drive DMs for a free "script" resource.

---

## Executive Summary

A 20-year-old shares his system for generating $30,000/month using Claude AI to create and sell a $1,000 digital product. The strategy relies on two Claude-powered workflows: (1) packaging personal skills into a sellable digital product using a prompt script, and (2) generating targeted video content to attract ideal buyers rather than chasing viral views. The creator offers the prompt script for free via Instagram DM to drive engagement and build his pipeline.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Quality over virality:** Videos with 3,000 views can outperform videos with 3 million views in revenue — targeting the right audience matters more than mass reach.
- **AI-packaged digital product:** Use a specific Claude prompt script to identify and package your existing skills/knowledge into a $1,000 digital product — no "special" skill required.
- **Targeted content strategy:** Ask Claude which topics to cover in your videos to attract your ideal buyer, not a general audience.
- **Competitor inspiration workflow:** Find 10 creators in your niche, feed their viral video scripts into Claude, and generate 10 derivative video scripts with similar angles.
- **Simple math to $1K/day:** 3 videos/day × ~500 views each = ~1,500 views/day. Convert just 1 viewer per day at $1,000 → $1,000 profit/day.
- **Low time investment:** The creator runs this system in 2–3 hours/day while attending university.

## Narrative & Step-by-Step Breakdown

**Beginning (Hook):** Opens with a provocative admission — "It may be unethical, but I use Claude to make $30,000 a month" — immediately signaling controversy and a money-making reveal.

**Middle (Core System):**
1. Copy-paste a specific prompt script into Claude → Claude asks you questions about your skills/experience → outputs a packaged digital product concept you can sell for $1,000.
2. Return to Claude and ask what video topics will attract your ideal buyer — not viral content, but targeted content.
3. Find 10 competitors monetizing a similar skill, copy-paste their viral video scripts into Claude, and prompt Claude to write 10 video scripts inspired by those ideas.
4. Post 3 videos/day. At ~500–600 views each, you need just 1 conversion/day at $1,000 = $1,000 profit.

**End (CTA):** Reiterates that no $10K course is being sold. Directs viewers to save the video and DM "script" on Instagram to receive the free prompt.
