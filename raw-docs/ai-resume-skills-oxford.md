# Oxford Study: AI Skills That Boost Resume Interview Rates by 15%

**Topic/Category:** Career Advice / Job Search / AI Skills
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To inform job seekers about a University of Oxford study that found adding specific AI-related phrases to a resume increases interview chances by 15%, and to show exactly what those phrases look like for three different career fields.

## Executive Summary

A University of Oxford experiment involving over 1,700 recruiters demonstrated that candidates who listed AI skills on their resumes saw a 15% increase in interview rates compared to those who didn't. The video breaks down field-specific "power phrases" for three roles — office admin, graphic design, and software engineering — emphasizing that calling out concrete AI tools and framing yourself as someone who *builds with AI* is the key differentiator recruiters responded to.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Oxford-backed data:** The 15% interview rate increase came from a real experiment with 1,700+ recruiters comparing AI-skilled vs. non-AI-skilled resumes.
- **Specificity matters:** Vague AI claims don't work — the study's resumes named specific tools (e.g., Midjourney, Adobe Firefly, prompt engineering).
- **Three fields covered:** Office administration, graphic design, and software engineering each had tailored "power phrases" that drove the uplift.
- **Graphic design is high-risk:** The creator flags that graphic designers may be among the first fields displaced by AI — making AI proficiency on the resume especially critical.
- **Core framing principle:** The phrase structure centers on "building with AI," not just "using AI" — this distinction resonated most with recruiters.
