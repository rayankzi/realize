## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Join Niche Organizations:** Join clubs or organizations aligned with your academic passions. These groups often maintain their own extensive alumni directories, which are easier to navigate than general school-wide databases.
- **Efficiency via Templates:** Create a concise email template to handle a high volume of outreach, but ensure it includes specific details about why you are reaching out to that particular individual.
- **Value Respect:** Respect the alum's time by having a clear goal for the interaction (e.g., general advice, referral, or interview tips) before reaching out.
- **Humanize the Interaction:** During coffee chats, balance professional questions with personal conversation. Alumni respond better to genuine human interaction than robotic, purely transactional dialogues.
- **Lead with Flexibility:** Have a plan for leading the conversation but be prepared to adapt based on the other person's "vibe."

- **Step 3: Preparation:** Define specific goals for each contact to avoid wasting time.
