## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Framework Familiarity:** Installing a modern framework like React gives beginners a starting point for building frontends and backends.
- **Resourceful Learning:** Use YouTube tutorials specifically tailored to your hardware (computer model) for initial setup.
- **The Debugging Mindset:** Debugging is a core part of the hackathon experience. Knowing where to look for answers is more important than knowing everything upfront.
- **Leverage AI and Communities:** Stack Overflow, Reddit, Geeks for Geeks, ChatGPT, and GitHub Copilot are essential tools for resolving errors.
- **Perseverance is Key:** The most important skill at a hackathon is the ability to keep trying different solutions until an error is resolved.
