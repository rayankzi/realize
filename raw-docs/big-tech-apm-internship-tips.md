# How to Land 3x Big Tech Product Manager Internships

**Topic/Category:** Career Development / Product Management
**Primary Goal of the Post:** To share the specific interview and research strategies used to secure multiple Product Manager internships at Microsoft, specifically targeting students from non-target schools.

---

## Executive Summary

Skyler (@studentbigtech) shares her journey of landing three Product Manager internships at Microsoft despite coming from a non-target school with no prior connections. The post highlights unconventional strategies like the "STAR Sandwich" for interviews, deep interviewer research on LinkedIn, and intensive company value alignment. It aims to demystify the APM (Associate Product Manager) recruitment process for ambitious women in tech.

## Key Insights & Main Ideas

- **The "STAR Sandwich" Method:** Move beyond the basic STAR method by adding opening and closing liners to provide context and demonstrate value.
- **Interviewer Research:** Use LinkedIn to "stalk" interviewers to prepare personalized questions about their journey, the product, and their role.
- **Strategic Follow-ups:** Always send a thank you note on LinkedIn within 24 hours of the interview.
- **Value Alignment:** Deeply research the company to explain *exactly* why you want to work there, how you align with their values, and how you provide value to specific products.
- **Overcoming Background Limitations:** Coming from a non-target school or having no connections is not an absolute barrier if you utilize high-leverage preparation techniques.

## Detailed Breakdown / Step-by-Step

- **Slide 1: Hook:** "How I landed 3x Product Manager Internships @ Microsoft" (non-target uni, no connections, no experience). Shows proof with offer letters/congratulations emails.
- **Slide 2: The STAR Sandwich:**
    - Don't just use STAR (Situation, Task, Action, Result).
    - Avoid diving directly into the story.
    - **Method:** Have an opening and closing liner that provides context on the situation and a value you'll provide to the company.
- **Slide 3: Interviewer Research:**
    - Research interviewers on LinkedIn beforehand.
    - Prepare 3 questions based on: 1. Their journey, 2. The product they work on, 3. Their current role.
    - Follow up with a LinkedIn thank you note within 24 hours.
- **Slide 4: Deep Company Research:**
    - Know exactly why you want Microsoft, why you align with their core values, and why Product Management specifically.
    - Research specific products and how you'd provide value to them.
    - **Action Item:** Write down 3 reasons why you want to work at the company before the interview.
