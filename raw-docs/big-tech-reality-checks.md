## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Soft skills beat hard skills:** Writing clean code is the baseline — communication, collaboration, and self-promotion are what actually drive career growth.
- **LeetCode is just a gatekeeper:** Grinding algorithms gets you in the door, but has little to do with the real day-to-day work of a great engineer.
- **ROI is owned by the company:** Shipping a feature that made $200M doesn't mean you get $200M — a crystal plaque is the reminder that you're an employee, not an equity partner.
- **Salaries have a ceiling:** Big Tech pays well, but it's still time-for-money. Building someone else's dream may not build generational wealth.
- **Imposter syndrome is a lie:** Most devs at top companies aren't superhuman — many just had the right timing, geography, or opportunities.
- **Team matching is luck:** Getting a bad manager or team is part of the game; adaptability matters more than the hand you're dealt.
