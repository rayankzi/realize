# How to Break into Product Management Without a CS Degree

**Topic/Category:** Career Advice / Product Management
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To provide a actionable 3-step framework (plus a bonus tip) for non-technical individuals to transition into product management roles.

---

## Executive Summary

The creator shares their personal journey of breaking into product management (PM) two years after college without a CS degree, engineering internships, or prior PM experience. The strategy focuses on getting into the tech ecosystem first, leveraging internal transitions through relationship building, and developing deep domain knowledge. A modern "bonus tip" highlights the power of "vibe coding" (using AI to build functional projects) to demonstrate domain expertise and attract recruiters on LinkedIn.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Lowering the Barrier:** You don't need a computer science degree or engineering background to become a PM.
- **Proximity is Power:** Being inside a tech company in any role (Sales, Marketing, CS) is the most effective first step.
- **Internal Mobility:** Trust is easier to build internally. Coffee chats with hiring managers are crucial for understanding role requirements and building advocates.
- **Domain Knowledge > Technical Skills:** Understanding customer pain points, market trends, and industry insights is the core value of a PM.
- **Show, Don't Just Tell:** Building a functional side project (even with AI/vibe coding) in a specific niche (e.g., FinTech) provides tangible proof of domain knowledge.

## Narrative & Step-by-Step Breakdown

### The 3-Step PM Transition Framework

1.  **Break into Tech First:**
    - Find a role adjacent to product management (Sales, Marketing, Engineering, Customer Success).
    - Goal: Get into the "ecosystem" to overcome the competitive barriers of PM roles.
2.  **Internal Move & Coffee Chats:**
    - Set up weekly coffee chats with hiring managers and current PMs.
    - Ask questions: "What are you looking for?" and "What does it take to do the job well?"
    - Leverage existing company trust to build relationships.
3.  **Develop Domain Knowledge:**
    - Gain industry insights by working in your current role and talking to customers.
    - Identify pain points and form opinions on market trends.
    - Positioning: Become the "voice of the customer" to build trust with product teams.

### Bonus Tip: The "Vibe Coding" Strategy
- Use AI/vibe coding to build a niche project (e.g., a personal finance app for FinTech roles).
- Get a few sign-ups to show user validation.
- Share the process and results on LinkedIn to attract recruiters and demonstrate domain expertise.
