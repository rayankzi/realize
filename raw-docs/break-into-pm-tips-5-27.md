# 3 Steps to Break Into Product Management (Without a CS Degree)

**Topic/Category:** Career Advice / Product Management
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To provide a strategic roadmap for aspiring product managers to break into the field by leveraging adjacent roles, internal networking, and domain expertise.

## Executive Summary

The video features a creator who successfully transitioned into product management (PM) two years post-graduation without a computer science degree, software engineering internships, or prior PM experience. Drawing from their own experience and mentoring hundreds of others, they outline a three-pillar strategy: breaking into the tech ecosystem through adjacent roles, leveraging internal networking and coffee chats for trust-building, and developing deep domain knowledge. The analysis highlights that being the "voice of the customer" is the ultimate value a PM provides, often outweighing technical credentials.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Enter Tech First:** Breaking into PM is highly competitive. Getting into a tech company in an adjacent role (Sales, Marketing, Engineering, Customer Success) is a crucial first step to "get in the game."
- **Internal Transitions are Strategic:** Moving into a PM role is easier from within a company because you have already established trust and built a reputation.
- **The "Coffee Chat" Strategy:** Consistency is key. Weekly coffee chats with hiring managers and current PMs help you understand the role requirements and build critical internal relationships.
- **Domain Knowledge Over Degrees:** A deep understanding of customer pain points, industry trends, and market direction is more valuable for a PM than a technical background.
- **Showcase Expertise Digitally:** Building niche-specific projects (e.g., a personal finance app for fintech) and sharing the journey on LinkedIn can attract recruiters directly.
