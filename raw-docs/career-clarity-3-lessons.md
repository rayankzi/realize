# 3 Things That Finally Clarify What You Want in Your Career

**Topic/Category:** Career Advice / Personal Development
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To provide ambitious, conventionally smart people with a framework for gaining career clarity by sharing three hard-won lessons from a year spent in consulting, an AI startup, and social media.

## Executive Summary

The creator distills a year of diverse professional experience — consulting, working at a San Francisco AI startup, and making tens of thousands of dollars online — into three key realizations that explain why most people stay confused about what they want to do with their careers. The core message is that clarity doesn't come from planning or researching harder; it comes from stepping outside familiar environments, letting the excitement of new experiences settle, and shedding the pursuit of prestige for its own sake.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Insight 1: Social bubbles warp your values.** Staying inside the same school, company, or industry for too long creates a distorted sense of what you should want. Physical and professional distance is required to gain an honest outside perspective.
- **Insight 2: Novelty masks reality.** Any new job or role comes with 2–3 months of excitement that makes it feel meaningful. True self-knowledge only appears once that novelty wears off and you can assess whether you genuinely enjoy the work — or just enjoyed looking like you do.
- **Insight 3: Prestige is a conditional tool, not a universal goal.** Chasing status makes sense only in systems where status directly drives success (corporate ladders, VC fundraising). Outside those systems, optimizing for a title or prestigious name is a costly distraction from doing work that actually interests you and where you can create real value.
