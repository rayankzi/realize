# The Career Documentation System: How to Protect Your Job and Get Promoted

**Topic/Category:** Career Development / Corporate Life
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To teach professionals a systematic approach to documenting their workplace contributions to ensure they are recognized during performance reviews and protected against organizational changes.

---

## Executive Summary

Your manager, HR, and leadership will not track your specific contributions or remember your "wins" by the end of the year. To control your career narrative and secure promotions, you must transition from relying on their memory to providing undeniable data. By maintaining a "brag sheet," saving evidence of positive feedback, and creating a paper trail of management conversations, you ensure your impact is documented, quantified, and irrefutable.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Control the Narrative:** Documentation allows you to define your own value rather than leaving it to a manager's vague recollection.
- **Quantifiable Brag Sheets:** Every project, solved problem, and improved metric should be dated, quantified, and saved in a running document.
- **Evidence Preservation:** Positive feedback from clients, teammates, and leadership should be screenshotted or forwarded to a personal folder as permanent "proof" of impact.
- **Protective Paper Trails:** Recap emails after 1:1 meetings prevent shifting priorities or blame from affecting your standing.
- **Alignment on Expectations:** Documenting goals in writing at the start of the year prevents "moving goalposts" during performance reviews.

## Narrative & Step-by-Step Breakdown

- **Beginning:** Identify the problem—managers and HR won't track your value for you.
- **Middle (The "What to Track" System):**
    1. **The Brag Sheet:** Maintain a running doc of quantified wins.
    2. **Feedback Collection:** Save emails and Slack messages praising your work.
    3. **1:1 Management:** Send recap emails after every meeting to confirm agreements.
    4. **Goal Setting:** Get expectations in writing at the start of the year.
- **End:** Reiterate the mantra: "If it's not documented, it didn't happen."
