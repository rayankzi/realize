# 10 Career Fair Mistakes to Avoid for SWE Students

**Topic/Category:** Career Development / Job Search
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To provide actionable advice on common pitfalls students make at career fairs and how to stand out to recruiters.

---

## Executive Summary

This video outlines 10 critical mistakes students make during career fairs, ranging from technical preparation (lack of a personal website or multiple resumes) to interpersonal skills (talking in large groups, sounding like a robot). The core message emphasizes the importance of professional polish, strategic networking, and treating career fairs as an opportunity to build genuine connections rather than just transactional interactions.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Digital Presence is Key:** A personal website acts as a centralized portfolio that recruiters often revisit after the event.
- **Efficiency in Communication:** Short, punchy introductions are more memorable than long-winded ones.
- **Strategic Networking:** Avoid "group pitches"; seek one-on-one interactions to truly stand out.
- **Preparation Overload:** Bringing extra resumes and researching companies deeply shows a level of commitment that surface-level candidates lack.
- **Human Connection:** Authenticity and personality matter as much as technical skills; avoid sounding overly rehearsed.

## Narrative & Step-by-Step Breakdown

- **Beginning:** The video hooks the viewer by listing 10 common mistakes in rapid succession.
- **Middle:** Detailed breakdown of why each mistake is detrimental:
  1. **No Personal Website:** Prevents recruiters from seeing the full scope of your work easily.
  2. **Slow Intro:** Leads to recruiter fatigue and disinterest.
  3. **Group Talking:** Makes it nearly impossible to differentiate yourself.
  4. **Running Out of Resumes:** Signals poor preparation.
  5. **Lack of Research:** Makes your interest seem superficial.
  6. **Surface Level Questions:** Wastes the recruiter's time with easily Googleable facts.
  7. **No Follow-up:** Cuts the connection before it can develop.
  8. **No Personality:** Makes you forgettable.
  9. **No Goal:** Results in aimless interactions.
- **End:** The final point (Mistake #10) resets expectations—one interaction isn't the job; it's the beginning of a connection.
