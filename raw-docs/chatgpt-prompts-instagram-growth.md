## Verbatim Templates & Scripts

All seven prompts are designed to be copied directly into ChatGPT. Here they are verbatim:

**Prompt 1 — Viral Content Ideation:**

> "You are a social media strategist specialized in rapid growth. Analyze the latest 10 viral Instagram trends in [your niche] and create 5 short, explosive ideas with high share potential. Be specific and practical."

**Prompt 2 — Pain-to-Hook Conversion:**

> "List the 10 biggest pain points that the audience [describe audience] feels about [topic]. Turn each pain point into a hook of up to 10 words, ready to be used in Reels. Avoid clichés, be direct and provocative."

**Prompt 3 — Reels Script (30 seconds):**

> "Create a 30-second script for an Instagram Reel about [topic]. Structure: 1) Irresistible hook, 2) Short engaging story, 3) CTA to comment or save. Use short and simple sentences. No fluff."

**Prompt 4 — Social Proof Phrases:**

> "Take the result [insert result] and create 5 short phrases that communicate social proof and curiosity, perfect for on-screen text in videos. Keep maximum impact in up to 10 words."

**Prompt 5 — Save-Worthy Carousel Tips:**

> "List 5 practical and little-known tips about [topic]. Each tip must be a maximum of 12 words, clear and easy to apply. The goal is to create a carousel that's impossible not to save."

**Prompt 6 — Content Repurposing:**

> "Turn this text [paste text] into: 1. a 7-second Reels script, 2. a 5-slide carousel, 3. a static post with a powerful statement. Adapt the language and format for each one."

**Prompt 7 — Irresistible CTAs:**

> "Create 10 short, creative, and direct CTAs that encourage comments or saves on posts about [topic]. Avoid generic phrases, use curiosity triggers and immediate action."
