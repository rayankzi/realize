# How to Use Claude AI to Automate Your Entire Job Search

**Topic/Category:** Job Search, AI Productivity, Career Tools
**Primary Goal of the Post:** Demonstrate how to use Claude AI (free version) with connectors to automate job searching — finding listings, assessing skill gaps, and ranking roles — in minutes instead of hours.

---

## Executive Summary

This carousel walks through a real workflow the creator used to leverage Claude AI's free tier to supercharge a job search. By attaching a resume, enabling the Indeed and Microsoft Learn connectors, and running a single structured prompt, Claude autonomously searched for live job listings, pulled salaries and apply links, cross-referenced required skills with free Microsoft Learn resources, and delivered a side-by-side gap analysis ranking which roles to apply to first — all in about 10 minutes.

## Key Insights & Main Ideas

- **Free tier is powerful:** The creator was surprised by how much Claude's free version could do without any paid upgrade.
- **Stop applying manually:** Manual job applications are time-consuming; AI can do the heavy lifting of discovery and research.
- **Connectors are the key unlock:** Claude's "Connectors" feature (Indeed + Microsoft Learn) lets it search live job boards and learning platforms in real time — no copy-pasting required.
- **Skill gap analysis included:** Claude doesn't just find jobs — it cross-references job requirements against free learning resources and tells you exactly what skills you're missing and how to fill them.
- **Ranked recommendations:** Claude produces a prioritized list of which roles to apply to first based on fit with your resume.

## Verbatim Templates & Scripts

**The Claude Job Search Prompt (from Slide 3):**

> I'm looking for a job and need help with two things:
>
> 1. Find me 10 jobs on Indeed. I'm looking for "recruiter" roles in Chicago, IL. I want full-time positions with 6+ years of experience. Pull the listings and share the apply links.
> 2. For the top results, check what skills are required and search Microsoft Learn to find free learning resources or certifications that match those skill gaps. Then give me a side-by-side breakdown: what the job needs, what Microsoft Learn has to help me get there, and which roles I should apply to first based on fit.
