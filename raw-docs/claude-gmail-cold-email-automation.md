# Cold Email Automation with Claude and Gmail

**Topic/Category:** Career Development / Job Search Strategy
**Primary Goal of the Post:** To teach users how to use Claude AI integrated with Gmail to automate high-converting cold outreach to founders in their specific industry.

---

## Executive Summary

This post outlines a strategy for overcoming the "spray and pray" approach to job applications by using Claude AI and the Gmail connector. By training Claude on your personal writing style from your "Sent" emails, you can generate concise, personalized outreach messages to top founders in your niche. The author reports a 7% response rate (7 responses from 100 emails) using this method, which is significantly higher than traditional cold applying.

## Key Insights & Main Ideas

- **Target High-Level Decision Makers:** Focus outreach on founders, directors, and VPs rather than general application portals.
- **Niche Alignment:** Reach out to people within your specific industry (e.g., e-commerce) to increase relevance.
- **Brevity is Key:** Keep outreach emails extremely short and specific.
- **AI Integration:** Use Claude's ability to read your "Sent" folder to mimic your voice and style, making AI-generated drafts feel authentic.
- **Iterative Optimization:** Repeat the process for 2 weeks and refine your templates based on response data.

## Verbatim Templates & Scripts

### Claude Integration Prompt
> "can you learn from my "sent" emails to learn how exactly i write, i like to keep it concise and then draft an email for me to (find them using web search : top-5 founders that are aggressively hiring in [your industry] space that i should definitely be reaching out to)?"

### Scaling Prompt Tip
> "repeat the same process, asking claude to exclude startups you already reached out to (through your "sent")"
