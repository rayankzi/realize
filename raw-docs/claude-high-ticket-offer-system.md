# How Claude Made Me $44,000 Last Month

**Topic/Category:** AI-Powered Business Strategy / Digital Products
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To teach viewers how to use a specific Claude prompt to package their skills into high-ticket digital products and how to attract buyers using niche content.

---

## Executive Summary

The creator, a 20-year-old entrepreneur, explains how he earns over $40,000 per month using Claude to build and sell high-ticket digital products. The strategy involves a two-step AI-driven process: first, using a comprehensive "interview" prompt in Claude to identify and package personal expertise into a $1,000+ offer; and second, using Claude to generate niche-specific content ideas that attract high-intent buyers rather than broad, viral audiences.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **AI as a Product Architect:** Claude can be used as an "expert high-ticket offer architect" to extract valuable skills from your life experience and structure them into a saleable product.
- **Micro-Niche Content Strategy:** Chasing viral views is less effective for sales than posting "micro-specific" videos that speak directly to an ideal client.
- **Conversion over Volume:** A small number of views (500–1,000 per video) from the right audience can lead to significant revenue if the product is priced correctly (high-ticket).
- **Accessibility:** The system is presented as requiring only a phone, Wi-Fi, and 2-3 hours of work per day, making it suitable for students or those without technical backgrounds.

## Narrative & Step-by-Step Breakdown

- **Beginning (The Hook):** The creator contrasts common "wasteful" uses of Claude (emails, therapy) with his $44,000/month system.
- **The "Interview" Phase:**
    1. Copy a specific JSON-formatted system prompt into Claude.
    2. Claude conducts a step-by-step interview covering background, skill extraction, market alignment, and offer architecture.
    3. The result is a packaged high-ticket digital product ($1,000+).
- **The Content Phase:**
    1. Ask Claude for video topics designed to attract the specific audience for the new product.
    2. Focus on "micro-niche" videos that solve specific problems for ideal clients.
- **The Math of Success:**
    - Aim for 500-1,000 views per video.
    - Post 3 videos a day (minimum 1,500 total views).
    - Converting just one person out of a thousand results in high-ticket sales.
- **End (Call to Action):** Encourages viewers to DM the word "script" for the prompt.
