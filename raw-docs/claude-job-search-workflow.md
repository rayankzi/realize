# How to Use Claude AI to Run Your Job Search (Free Workflow)

**Topic/Category:** Job Search / AI Productivity
**Primary Goal of the Post:** Show job seekers how to use Claude's free tier — with connectors like Indeed and Microsoft Learn — to automate job discovery, skill gap analysis, and application prioritization in under 10 minutes.

---

## Executive Summary

The creator demonstrates a free Claude AI workflow that replaces hours of manual job searching. By attaching a resume and using a specific prompt with Claude's Indeed and Microsoft Learn connectors enabled, Claude autonomously finds 10 live job listings, reads descriptions and salaries, identifies skill gaps, and cross-references Microsoft Learn for free certifications — then delivers a side-by-side breakdown recommending which roles to apply to first based on fit.

## Key Insights & Main Ideas

- **The free version is sufficient:** This entire workflow runs on Claude's free tier — no paid subscription required.
- **Connectors are the secret:** Claude's native connectors (Indeed, Microsoft Learn) let it search live job listings and learning platforms in real-time — no copy-pasting required.
- **Resume as context:** Attaching your PDF resume to the conversation lets Claude personalize job recommendations and skill gap analysis to your actual background.
- **One prompt does everything:** A single, well-crafted prompt handles job discovery, description reading, skill gap identification, and role prioritization all at once.
- **Time savings are dramatic:** A task that would take ~1 hour manually takes about 10 minutes with this workflow.

## Verbatim Templates & Scripts

**The Claude Prompt (shown in Slide 3):**

> I'm looking for a job and need help with two things:
>
> 1. Find me 10 jobs on Indeed, I'm looking for "recruiter" roles in Chicago, IL. I want full-time positions with 6+ years of experience. Pull the listings and share the apply links.
> 2. For the top results, check what skills are required and search Microsoft Learn to find free learning resources or certifications that match those skill gaps.
>
> Then give me a side-by-side breakdown: what the job needs, what Microsoft Learn has to help me get there, and which roles I should apply to first based on fit.

*Usage instructions from the carousel: Attach your resume (PDF) before sending this prompt. Enable the Indeed and Microsoft Learn connectors in Claude's Connectors menu before running.*
