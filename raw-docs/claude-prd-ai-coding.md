# How to Write a Product Spec (PRD) with Claude

**Topic/Category:** AI Coding & Product Management
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To teach viewers how to use Claude to create a Product Requirements Document (PRD) to ensure technical projects have a clear direction before coding begins.

---

## Executive Summary

Many AI coders make the mistake of building without a clear plan. This reel, part of the "Building with Claude Code" series (Day 12), explains how a software engineer and former PM uses Claude to draft a PRD. By defining the problem, user base, and desired outcomes first, developers can use Claude as a co-working partner to refine core features and maintain a "living document" that guides the entire lifecycle of a product.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **The Biggest Mistake:** Building software without knowing exactly what is being built or why.
- **What is a PRD?:** A written document that answers:
    - What problem are we solving?
    - What are we building to solve it?
    - What does a successful outcome look like?
- **Claude as a Co-worker:** Use Claude to brainstorm and filter features, ensuring you focus on the "minimum set of features" (MVP) rather than a "Christmas wish list."
- **Living Document:** A PRD should never be "write-it-once-and-forget-it." It must adapt as the product grows and bridge design, business, and engineering.
- **Strategy Over Format:** The value of a PRD is the thinking and strategizing it requires, not the specific template used.
