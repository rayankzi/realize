# 6 Pro Prompting Tips for Anthropic Claude

**Topic/Category:** Prompt Engineering / AI Tools
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To provide viewers with actionable tips to improve their prompting results in Claude and avoid "AI slop."

## Executive Summary

This reel outlines six specific strategies to enhance interactions with Anthropic's Claude. By moving away from vague instructions and adopting structured frameworks like XML tags and role-playing, users can achieve significantly more accurate, professional, and useful outputs. The content emphasizes that specificity and structure are the keys to unlocking Claude's full potential.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Specificity Over Vagueness:** Vague prompts lead to vague, "slop" results. Direct, specific instructions are essential.
- **Structured Frameworks (XML):** Claude is specifically trained to understand and follow instructions better when they are wrapped in XML tags (e.g., `<role>`, `<context>`, `<rules>`).
- **Cognitive Priming:** Explicitly telling Claude to "think hard," "stay factual," or "make no mistakes" improves the intelligence and accuracy of the output.
- **The "Role" Factor:** Assigning Claude a professional persona (e.g., Senior Product Designer) sets the tone and depth of the interaction.
- **Example-Based Guardrails:** Providing "few-shot" examples of both good and bad outputs helps Claude understand the desired boundaries.
- **Format Control:** Explicitly defining the output format (tables, bulleted lists) eliminates guesswork.
