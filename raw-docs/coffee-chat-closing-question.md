# The One Coffee Chat Question That Actually Differentiates You

**Topic/Category:** Career Advice / Networking / Early Career
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To teach early-career candidates a more personalized, thoughtful closing question to ask at the end of coffee chats — replacing generic questions with one that generates uniquely valuable advice.

---

## Executive Summary

Most people end coffee chats with tired, generic questions like "Is there anyone else I should speak to?" or "What should I study for interviews?" While proactive in spirit, these questions are vague and can even put the other person in an awkward position. This reel introduces a single, more powerful replacement question that prompts the contact to reflect on your specific background and give advice tailored to you — making the conversation far more memorable and valuable.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Generic closing questions fall flat:** "Is there someone else I should talk to?" puts the contact on the spot and rarely yields unique insight.
- **Personalization is the differentiator:** A question tied to *your* specific background and conversation forces a more thoughtful, individualized response.
- **The better question reframes the dynamic:** Instead of asking for a referral or generic tips, you're asking the contact to mentally step into your shoes — making their advice far more relevant.
- **Quality over quantity:** One great, tailored answer is more valuable than repeating the same generic coffee chat with multiple people.

## Narrative & Step-by-Step Breakdown

- **Beginning (Hook):** Opens by calling out the most common coffee chat closing question — "Is there anyone else I should speak to?" — and acknowledging why people ask it (it seems proactive).
