## Executive Summary

This reel emphasizes the importance of coffee chats as a critical component of a modern job search strategy. It provides three distinct outreach templates tailored for different networking scenarios: alumni connections, shared interests, and career pivots. The core strategy involves leveraging LinkedIn's filtering tools to prioritize contacts with shared backgrounds, making outreach more personable and effective.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Networking is Essential:** In the current job market, coffee chats are a "must" to stand out.
- **Strategic Filtering:** Use LinkedIn filters to find people with shared attributes (school, organization, major, background) to increase response rates.
- **Personalization Matters:** Successful outreach is personable and identifies a specific connection point rather than being a generic request.
- **Targeted Approach:** Focus on three main outreach categories: Alumni/School, Shared Interests, and Intentional Pivots.

## Verbatim Templates & Scripts

### 1. Alumni / School Connection
> Hi <Name>, My name is <Name> and I’m currently a <Year> at <College>. As a fellow <Mascot>, I’d love to connect and learn more about your path into <Role/Company>. Would you be open to a quick chat sometime?

### 2. Shared Interest / Specific Background
> Hi xx! I saw you were also involved in <Club/Org> as well. I would love to hear how that experience helped shape your career.

### 3. Intentional Pivot / Targeted Outreach
> Hi <Name>, I’m currently working in <Current Field/Company> and am interested in moving into <Target Role/Company/Industry>. I came across your profile and noticed that you made a similar transition from <X> to <Y>, which really stood out to me. If you’d be open to it, I’d love to hear more about your experience and how you navigated that shift.
