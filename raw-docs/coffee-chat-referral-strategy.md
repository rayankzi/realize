# Stop Applying to Hundreds of Jobs: The Coffee Chat Strategy

**Topic/Category:** Career Advice / Job Search Strategy
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To persuade university students to shift their focus from high-volume cold applications to consistent networking and coffee chats to secure referrals.

---

## Executive Summary

The creator, self-described as a "lazy university student," reveals their unconventional approach to landing internship interviews without mass-applying. By leveraging the power of "creating your own luck" through at least one weekly coffee chat with interesting individuals, they have transitioned from a cold application process to a referral-based system. The video emphasizes that while the odds of a resume being selected from a pool of thousands are nearly zero, a referral almost guarantees an interview.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Quality Over Quantity:** Applying to hundreds of internships is often less effective than targeted networking.
- **Consistency in Networking:** Aim for at least one coffee chat per week with someone you find interesting, regardless of their position.
- **Create Your Own Luck:** Referrals and opportunities (like being invited to meet a startup team in Paris) are the result of proactive outreach, not random chance.
- **The Referral Advantage:** A referral bypasses the high-volume resume filtering process, significantly increasing the likelihood of securing an interview.
- **Humanizing Leadership:** Realize that CEOs and high-level executives are approachable and "regular people" once you initiate a conversation.

## Narrative & Step-by-Step Breakdown

- **Beginning:** The hook—the creator admits to being "lazy" and not applying to hundreds of jobs, yet still getting weekly interviews.
- **Middle:** While traveling to Paris to meet a startup CEO, the creator explains the philosophy of "creating your own luck." They reveal their strategy: coffee chatting one new person every week.
- **End:** The creator explains the "why"—cold application success rates are near zero, whereas a referral is a direct path to an interview.
