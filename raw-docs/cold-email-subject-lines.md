# This Cold Email Subject Line Got Me Hired

**Topic/Category:** Cold Emailing, Job Applications, Marketing Outreach
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** Share the exact cold email subject lines the creator used to land a job, plus provide additional high-performing subject line templates viewers can copy.

---

## Executive Summary

This short-form reel reveals the creator's personal cold email strategy that led to getting hired. The video showcases two specific subject lines the creator actually used — one formal ("Letter Of Intent || Brand Manager") and one personality-driven ("Shagun, Not Lifafa—But Definitely Worth Opening!") — before providing a list of eight additional proven subject line templates that viewers can adapt for their own cold outreach, whether for job applications, networking, or marketing.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Insight 1:** A strong cold email subject line is critical for getting your email opened — the creator attributes getting hired directly to their subject line strategy.
- **Insight 2:** Two approaches work: a professional/formal format (e.g., "Letter Of Intent || Brand Manager") and a creative/personality-driven format (e.g., cultural references that show wit and make the recipient curious).
- **Insight 3:** Personalization and curiosity-driving language are recurring themes across all the recommended subject lines — using the recipient's first name, referencing something real, or posing a question all increase open rates.
- **Insight 4:** The subject lines span multiple use cases — job applications, sales outreach, networking requests, and general cold emails — making them versatile templates.
