# Cold Email Templates That Actually Land Interviews

**Topic/Category:** Job Search / Networking / Career Development
**Primary Goal of the Post:** Share four battle-tested cold email and LinkedIn DM templates for job seekers — covering post-application outreach, general networking, follow-ups, and post-conversation referral asks — with the goal of helping applicants break through the silence and get responses.

---

## Executive Summary

Creator @advicewithjean "un-gatekeeps" the exact cold email and DM scripts she personally used to start conversations, secure referrals, and land interviews in tech. The carousel presents four distinct templates mapped to the most common outreach scenarios a job seeker faces: following up after applying, building a network from scratch, nudging someone who went quiet, and converting a coffee chat into an ongoing relationship. Each template is low-pressure, personalized by design, and conversational in tone — prioritizing warmth over desperation.

## Key Insights & Main Ideas

- **Personalization is non-negotiable:** Every template includes prompts to reference specific products, teams, or career moments — generic messages don't work.
- **Short and humble wins:** None of the templates are long. They lead with value or genuine curiosity, and always give the recipient an out.
- **Only follow up once:** The carousel explicitly warns against multiple follow-ups — send one, then move on.
- **Platform-agnostic:** Templates are labeled for use via email *or* LinkedIn DM, making them flexible for wherever the contact lives.
- **Referral ask is soft, not transactional:** The warm referral template focuses on staying in touch and mutual value, not an explicit "refer me" ask.

## Verbatim Templates & Scripts

**Template 1 — Applied to a Job Posting (Email or LinkedIn DM):**

> Hi [Name],
>
> I just applied for the [Role - include link to JD] and wanted to reach out directly. I'm excited about [Company]'s work on [specific product, team, or initiative].
>
> I've spent the last [X years] working in [relevant area], where I've [impact, metric, or responsibility]. I'd love to bring that experience to your team.
>
> Hoping you might be able to flag my name to the recruiting / hiring team.
>
> Thanks so much for your time and considering my request!
>
> Best,
> [Your Name]

---

**Template 2 — General Networking / Industry Exploration:**

> Hi [Name],
>
> I came across your profile while learning more about [industry / role], and your career path really stood out to me.
>
> I'm currently [your situation], and would love to hear how you've approached [specific part of their journey].
>
> No pressure at all but if you're open to sharing insights on [X] or [Y] I'd really appreciate it. If not, thank you anyways for leading the way!
>
> Thanks,
> [Your Name]

---

**Template 3 — Follow-Up (After No Response):**

> Hi [Name],
>
> Just following up in case my last message got buried. Totally understand if now isn't the right time — appreciate you either way!
>
> Best,
> [Your Name]
>
> *PS. Only follow up once. Then move on.*

---

**Template 4 — Warm Referral / Post-Conversation Ask:**

> Hi [Name], Thanks again for taking the time to chat. I really appreciated your insights on [specific topic].
>
> If it makes sense, I'd love to stay in touch and explore how I could potentially support [team / company] in the future. I want to pay it forward :)
>
> Grateful for the conversation either way!
