# How to Land Consulting Internships Without Prior Experience

**Topic/Category:** Career Advice / Management Consulting
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To demystify management consulting and provide actionable strategies for landing internships at top firms without having a traditional background or prior experience.

---

## Executive Summary

This reel breaks down the path to landing management consulting internships for those without relevant experience. The creator emphasizes that while many obsess over case interviews, the "fit" portion is where candidates often eliminate themselves. By understanding that firms are looking for "teammates, not textbooks," and preparing with an "unhinged level of preparedness," candidates can overcome a lack of prior experience. The analysis covers what management consulting actually is, how to approach fit interviews, and a clever LinkedIn hack for case preparation.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Consulting is Problem-Solving in Chaos:** It's defined as being called in to solve a company's "messy, expensive problems" (e.g., declining profits, expansion strategy, acquisitions).
- **The "Fit" Interview is a Teammate Test:** Firms aren't just looking for impressive resumes; they want to know if they can work with you in a high-pressure environment for long hours.
- **Focus on Impact over Titles:** Instead of just listing titles (e.g., "I was President"), use a problem-action-result framework that highlights leadership, collaboration, and calmness under pressure.
- **Research the Interviewer's Specialty:** Cases are often inspired by the interviewer's actual work. Stalking them on LinkedIn to find their industry focus (e.g., healthcare) can give you a significant advantage.
- **Preparation Beats Experience:** Curiosity and a high level of preparedness can compensate for a lack of prior consulting-specific experience or case competition wins.
