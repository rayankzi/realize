# The Brag Sheet: Your Corporate Career Cheat Code

**Topic/Category:** Career Development / Tech Careers
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To teach viewers how to document their work achievements using a "brag sheet" to accelerate career growth and promotion opportunities.

---

## Executive Summary

Michael, a tech career expert, introduces the "brag sheet" as an essential tool for documenting professional accomplishments. He emphasizes that since no one else is responsible for your career growth, individuals must take ownership of documenting their impact. The reel provides a structured four-step framework for creating a brag sheet that aligns current responsibilities with promotion requirements, backed by empirical proof and business metrics.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Self-Advocacy is Mandatory:** The "Reality is no one's coming to save us," so you must invest in documenting your own growth.
- **The "Delta" Strategy:** Success is defined by the gap (delta) between your current role's responsibilities and the next level's requirements.
- **Proof-Based Documentation:** Qualitative feedback (Slack/Email) and quantitative impact (Metrics/Dashboards) are equally vital.
- **Consistency over Intensity:** Updating the sheet for 30 minutes every 2-3 weeks is more effective than trying to recall a year's worth of work during a performance review.
- **Ownership > Participation:** Leading five projects is significantly more valuable on a brag sheet than collaborating on a thousand.

## Narrative & Step-by-Step Breakdown

1. **Leveraling Guidelines:** Identify your company's official responsibility descriptions for your current and target roles.
2. **The Three-Column Sheet:**
   - **Column 1:** Current responsibilities.
   - **Column 2:** Next-level responsibilities.
   - **Column 3:** The "Delta" (the difference/requirements for promotion).
3. **Gather Validation:** Collect screenshots of positive feedback from Slack, emails, and one-on-one meetings to prove peer and leadership support.
4. **Project Ownership:** Document projects where you were the primary lead rather than just a collaborator.
5. **Business Impact:** Link your work to specific metrics (Top of funnel: website visits/impressions; Bottom of funnel: revenue).
