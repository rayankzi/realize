# The Best Cover Letter Structure That Actually Gets You Noticed

**Topic/Category:** Career Advice / Job Search
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To break down a simple, structured cover letter format that stands out to hiring managers by being specific, measurable, and straight to the point — saving applicants time while maximizing impact.

---

## Executive Summary

A hiring manager reveals the best cover letter he's ever seen and breaks it down step by step. The format ditches fluffy openers and vague soft-skill claims in favor of three simple elements: a direct opener naming the job and company, three key skills backed by real numbers, and a brief enthusiastic close. The caption reinforces this with a clean numbered structure and even mentions a Reslink video pitch as a bonus differentiator. The core message: keep it simple, structured, and specific — and spend no more than 5–10 minutes on it.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Skip the fluff opener:** Phrases like "I wanted to express my interest in…" add no value. Lead with the job title, company, and your fit in 2–3 sentences.
- **Three skills + proof is the formula:** Don't say you're "very operational" — show it. Pick three relevant skills and attach measurable results to each.
- **Numbers are non-negotiable:** Just like a CV, your cover letter needs impact metrics. Vague claims don't justify your ability to do the job.
- **Close with enthusiasm, not length:** End with a couple of lines about the company — don't overdo it. The whole letter should take under 5–10 minutes.
- **Reslink video pitches add a human layer:** A 1-minute video attached to the cover letter lets hiring managers see your personality and communication style — something a PDF cannot convey.

## Narrative & Step-by-Step Breakdown

**Beginning (Hook):** The creator opens as a hiring manager who hates cover letters — immediately establishing credibility and grabbing attention from job seekers frustrated with the process.

**Middle (Core Content):**
1. **The opener** — Mention the job title, company, and your fit in the first few sentences. Be direct.
2. **Three skills with proof** — Choose three relevant skills and pair each with a specific, measurable result:
   - Operations & process building → cut project timelines by 30%
   - Growth & go-to-market execution → drove 1,500 new users in 60 days
   - Fundraise & strategy support → contributed to a £250K pre-seed raise
3. **The close** — Express genuine enthusiasm about the company in 1–2 sentences. No more.
