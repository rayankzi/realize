# The System for Creative Consistency: How to Post Every Single Day

**Topic/Category:** Content Creation & Productivity
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To share a replicable system for maintaining creative consistency and avoiding "blank page syndrome" when creating content.

---

## Executive Summary

The creator shares a structured workflow developed over six months of daily posting to maintain creative consistency. The system relies on front-loading idea generation on Sundays, utilizing a Notion-based inspiration bank, and leveraging Claude AI to structure scripts before the actual writing process begins. By focusing on morning creative sessions and minimizing distractions, the creator has turned consistency into a repeatable skill.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Avoid the Blank Page:** Never start writing from scratch; maintain a running repository of inspiration (Notion doc) to pull from.
- **Dedicated Ideation Sessions:** Dedicate a specific time (like Sundays) for high-volume brain dumps (20+ ideas) to prime the brain for creativity.
- **AI-Assisted Structuring:** Use tools like Claude to "flesh out" concepts and create structures ("bones") for scripts before the actual writing phase.
- **Environment & Routine:** Incorporate non-work activities (matcha, nature, reading) to spark creativity before morning scripting sessions.
- **Deep Focus:** Minimize digital distractions by closing all unnecessary applications during the writing process.

## Narrative & Step-by-Step Breakdown

1. **The Inspiration Bank:** Maintain a Notion document with links, quotes, and concepts that spark interest.
2. **Sunday Brain Dump:** Write 20 ideas without stopping to enter "creative mode."
3. **Weekly Selection:** Choose the top 7 ideas for the upcoming week.
4. **AI Structuring:** Set up Claude conversations for each selected idea to draft structures and key takeaways.
5. **Morning Routine:** Script in the morning after activities like getting a matcha or reading.
6. **Focus Mode:** Close all apps except Claude and Notion to eliminate distractions.
