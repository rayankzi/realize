# Creative Consistency: A 6-Month System for Content Creation

**Topic/Category:** Content Creation & Productivity
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To share a repeatable system for maintaining creative consistency and scripting content efficiently using Notion and AI tools like Claude.

---

## Executive Summary

After committing to daily content creation for six months, the creator shares a refined workflow that moves from raw ideation to structured scripts. The system emphasizes never starting from a blank page, leveraging a "brain dump" technique every Sunday, and using intentional morning routines and focused digital environments (Claude and Notion) to eliminate distractions and spark creativity.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Never Start Blank:** Maintain a running repository of inspiration (videos, quotes, concepts) in a tool like Notion to ensure you always have a starting point.
- **Quantity Leads to Quality:** The "20 Ideas" exercise on Sundays forces the brain into "creative mode" and provides a large pool from which to select the best content.
- **Pre-Structure Everything:** Setting up the "bones" of a conversation or script before the actual writing day reduces friction and decision fatigue.
- **Optimize Your Environment:** Physical movement (going outside) and digital minimalism (closing unnecessary tabs) are practical "unlocks" for deep creative work.

## Narrative & Step-by-Step Breakdown

- **The Sunday Prep:**
    1. **Idea Generation:** Write 20 ideas without stopping to prime the brain.
    2. **Selection:** Pick the top 7 ideas from the brain dump and the existing Notion inspiration bank.
    3. **Structural Setup:** For each of the 7 ideas, set up a conversation (e.g., in Claude) and draft the basic structure and key takeaway.
- **The Daily Execution:**
    1. **Morning Ritual:** Start the day with activity outside of work (e.g., getting a matcha, reading a book) to spark creativity.
    2. **Focused Scripting:** Script in the morning.
    3. **Digital Discipline:** Close all applications except for the essential tools (Claude and Notion) to prevent distractions.
