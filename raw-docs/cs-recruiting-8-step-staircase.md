# The 8-Step CS Recruiting Staircase: Beyond "Just Build Projects"

**Topic/Category:** Career Development / CS Recruiting
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To debunk the "just build projects" advice as incomplete and provide a structured 8-step progression framework for landing roles in big tech and high-tier engineering.

---

## Executive Summary

The video argues that the common advice to "just build projects" is lazy and incomplete, leading to false confidence and rejection from top-tier companies. Using a "progressive overload" analogy from the gym, the creator explains that landing a role at a company like Google or Airbnb is like bench-pressing 225 lbs—you can't start there. Instead, candidates must climb an 8-step "staircase" of experience, starting from solo projects and moving through research, startups, and mid-level companies before reaching FAANG or Quant roles.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **The Problem with "Just Build Projects":** It lacks context and doesn't tell you *what* level of projects or experience you need for specific tiers of companies.
- **The Progressive Overload Analogy:** Just as you don't start at the gym with your max weight, you shouldn't expect to land a FAANG role with only a basic personal project.
- **The Error of Skipping Steps:** Most failures in CS recruiting occur because candidates try to skip multiple levels of the staircase (e.g., jumping from solo projects straight to FAANG).
- **Targeted Progression:** The key is to build the "right thing at the right stage" and respect the natural progression of career growth.
