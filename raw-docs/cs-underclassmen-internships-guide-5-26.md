# Essential Internships and Fellowships for CS Underclassmen

**Topic/Category:** Career Development / Computer Science / Internship Strategy
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To provide a comprehensive list of prestigious internship and fellowship opportunities specifically designed for college freshmen and sophomores in Computer Science.

---

## Executive Summary

The video serves as a roadmap for underclassmen (freshmen and sophomores) looking to secure early-career opportunities in tech. The creator, Ella Gonzales (a returning Microsoft intern), shares her personal success with the Microsoft Explore program and highlights several other major tech programs (Google STEP, Meta University). She distinguishes between internships and fellowships—noting that fellowships often focus more on mentorship and open-source contributions—and provides a definitive GitHub repository resource for viewers to find more opportunities.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Early Action is Key:** Freshmen and sophomores have access to exclusive "underclassmen" programs that serve as feeders for future junior/senior internships and full-time roles.
- **Personal Testimony:** The creator attributes her career success to completing a sophomore summer internship (Microsoft Explore), demonstrating the long-term ROI of these programs.
- **Program Status Updates:** 
    - **Microsoft Explore:** Still open and accepting freshmen.
    - **Google STEP:** Now rebranded/changed to Associate Software Developer Intern (ASDI).
    - **Meta University:** Reported as closed for the current cycle.
- **Fellowship Benefits:** Fellowships are often shorter but provide intense mentorship, training, and exposure to open-source contributions compared to traditional internships.
- **Centralized Resource:** A curated list of these opportunities is maintained on GitHub (see link below).
