## Executive Summary

This reel lays out a focused, no-fluff roadmap for anyone starting cybersecurity from scratch in 2026. The creator emphasizes that the biggest trap beginners fall into is trying to learn everything at once — hopping between certifications, watching random videos, and never building real skills. Instead, the roadmap boils down to: pick one path, build a proper foundation, start projects immediately, document and share your work publicly, and apply for jobs before you feel ready. The caption reinforces this with a concise 7-step summary that adds one extra point not emphasized in the audio: don't hide behind certifications.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Pick ONE lane early:** Trying to learn hacking, cloud, networking, malware, and coding all at once leads to overwhelm. Recommended beginner roles include SOC Analyst, Cybersecurity Analyst, GRC, and Third-Party Risk Analyst.
- **Build a structured foundation:** Start with a beginner cybersecurity course, then move to Security+ certification for real-world context on networks, attacks, defense, and risk.
- **Projects beat passive learning:** Don't wait until you feel "ready." Start building projects immediately — home labs, firewall configs, phishing email analysis, basic risk assessments.
- **Document and share everything:** Post projects on LinkedIn, build a portfolio, update your CV. Recruiters need proof, not a list of hours watched.
- **Don't hide behind certifications (from caption):** You don't need a stack of certs to begin applying and building experience.
- **Apply earlier than you think:** You'll never feel 100% ready. Apply while you're still learning — that's how real improvement happens.
