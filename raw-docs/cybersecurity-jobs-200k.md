## Executive Summary

This 8-slide carousel highlights six cybersecurity roles that collectively pay between $130K and $240K — all flagged as "UNDERRATED" because most applicants skip them. The post is designed for tech professionals and career switchers who may not know where the real money is in cybersecurity beyond the typical "ethical hacker" or generic IT security path. It closes with a concrete five-step action plan to get started, reinforcing a follow CTA for ongoing career intel.

## Key Insights & Main Ideas

- **Cloud Security Architect is the highest-paying role listed** ($180K–$240K), driven by the surge in cloud infrastructure migration to AWS, Azure, and GCP outpacing talent supply.
- **All six roles are labeled "UNDERRATED"** — meaning they tend to stay open longer and face less competition, which the post frames as a hiring opportunity.
- **AppSec Engineer bridges dev and security** ($160K–$220K), sitting at the DevSecOps intersection — valuable for engineers looking to transition without fully leaving software development.
- **Red Team Operator is the rarest role** ($150K–$210K) — specialized offensive security work that keeps roles open for months due to scarcity of experienced operators.
- **GRC Manager requires no heavy coding** ($130K–$190K) — making it an accessible high-salary path for those without a deep technical background, with growing demand driven by global regulation.
- **Security Architect is the most senior role** ($170K–$230K), requiring systems thinking and the ability to communicate risk at every organizational level.
- **Threat Intelligence Analyst pays a premium for depth** ($140K–$195K), tracking adversary behavior from criminal groups to nation-state actors using OSINT and threat intel skills.

**Slide 1 (Cover):** Bold hook — "Cybersecurity Jobs Paying $200k+" over a cinematic shot of a woman working on a laptop against a high-rise city view. Sets an aspirational, high-income framing.

**Slide 5 — Threat Intelligence Analyst ($140K–$195K):** Tracks adversary behavior — from criminal groups to nation-state actors — to help organizations get ahead of attacks proactively. Tags: THREAT INTEL, OSINT, ANALYST.

**Slide 6 — Security Architect ($170K–$230K):** Designs the overall security strategy for an organization. Senior role rewarding experience, systems thinking, and cross-level risk communication. Tags: ENTERPRISE, ARCHITECTURE, SENIOR LEVEL.

**Slide 7 — GRC Manager ($130K–$190K):** Governance, Risk, and Compliance — often overlooked early in careers but offers no heavy coding requirement, strong salary ceiling, and rising global demand. Tags: COMPLIANCE, RISK, NO CODING NEEDED.
