## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Prioritize Interactions:** High-end recruiters want to see more than static screens. Portfolios should include video walkthroughs, functional prototypes, and micro-interactions to demonstrate how the design actually feels and works.
- **Demonstrate "Vibe Coding":** In the age of AI, being able to "vibe code"—using AI tools and a modern tech stack to build or prototype—is a significant differentiator. It shows technical agility and a forward-thinking workflow.
- **Master the Design-to-Engineering Spectrum:** Designers should aim to be "in the middle" or closer to the engineering end of the spectrum (e.g., comfortable with Git and pushing to production) rather than just being confined to design tools like Figma.
- **Showcase Pure Craft:** Not every case study needs to be a complex business solution. At least one project should focus purely on "craft"—demonstrating exceptional aesthetic and technical execution.
