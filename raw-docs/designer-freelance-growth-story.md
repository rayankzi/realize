# From Zero to $29,000/Month: A Designer's Journey

**Topic/Category:** Business / Freelancing / Career Pivot
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To inspire aspiring designers and entrepreneurs by sharing a step-by-step journey of transitioning from a Product Manager role to running a successful 7-figure design agency.

---

## Executive Summary

The creator details their 5-year transition from having zero design experience to generating $29,000 a month (and eventually 7 figures annually). Starting as a Product Manager at Canva, they spent nights and weekends learning UI/UX, built a portfolio through free work for friends and family, and eventually scaled through LinkedIn outreach, shifting from hourly rates to monthly retainers.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Start Small & Free:** Building an initial portfolio often requires doing free work for family and friends to gain experience and tangible results.
- **Aggressive Outreach:** Scaling a freelance business requires active pitching. The creator scoured VC and accelerator websites to find founders on LinkedIn.
- **Pricing Strategy Shift:** Moving from hourly billing to monthly retainers is crucial for scaling. Hourly rates penalize efficiency; retainers reward expertise and faster decision-making.
- **Internal Transition:** Use your current job as a learning ground. The creator designed at work whenever possible while still employed as a PM.
- **The Power of Retainers:** Scaling from $50/hour to $17,000/month retainers allowed for team expansion and 7-figure revenue.

## Narrative & Step-by-Step Breakdown

1. **The Catalyst:** Working at Canva as a PM but feeling jealous of the design team.
2. **The Skill Build:** Learning UI/UX on nights and weekends; designing at work when possible.
3. **The Portfolio:** Designing for free for family and friends to create the first portfolio.
4. **The Hustle:** Messaging founders on LinkedIn by finding them via VC and accelerator directories.
5. **The First Win:** Landing a first paid project at $50/hour.
6. **The Pivot:** Realizing hourly pay was a bottleneck; moved to $3,000/month retainers.
7. **The Leap:** Resigning from Canva once freelance income matched the full-time salary.
8. **The Scale:** Three years later, charging $17,000/month with a team of five and 7-figure revenue.
