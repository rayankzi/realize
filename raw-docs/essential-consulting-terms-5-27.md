# Essential Consulting Terms You Need to Know

**Topic/Category:** Consulting / Finance / Career
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To educate students and aspiring consultants on 5 essential industry terms to sound like a professional during interviews and on the job.

## Executive Summary

This reel breaks down five fundamental concepts used daily in management consulting: MECE, Buy-In, Run Rate, Benchmarking, and "The So-What?". While the background music is lighthearted, the content is highly practical for anyone preparing for case interviews or a career in finance and consulting.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **MECE (Mutually Exclusive, Collectively Exhaustive):** A framework for structuring thoughts without overlap or gaps. If your structure isn’t MECE, interviewers will notice.
- **Buy-In:** The critical process of securing stakeholder agreement. A brilliant recommendation is useless without client commitment.
- **Run Rate:** Estimating annual value from a current metric (e.g., £100k/month equals a £1.2M run rate).
- **Benchmarking:** Comparing performance against industry peers or best-in-class competitors to identify and quantify gaps.
- **The So-What?:** The most important question in consulting. Every finding or number must have a clear implication and recommended action.
