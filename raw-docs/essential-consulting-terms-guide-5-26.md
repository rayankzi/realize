## Executive Summary

This content provides a quick breakdown of five fundamental concepts in management consulting: MECE, Buy-In, Run Rate, Benchmarking, and "The So-What?". The content aims to help students or aspiring consultants sound more professional and prepared for interviews or client work by defining critical jargon and its practical application.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **MECE (Mutually Exclusive, Collectively Exhaustive):** A foundational framework for organizing information so there are no overlapping points and no gaps.
- **Buy-In:** Emphasizes that brilliant recommendations are useless without the agreement and commitment of key stakeholders.
- **Run Rate:** A tool for annualizing current metrics (e.g., £100k/month = £1.2M run rate) to understand business scale.
- **Benchmarking:** The practice of comparing a client's performance against industry peers or best-in-class competitors.
- **The So-What?:** The most critical question in consulting, focusing on the implications and actions derived from data rather than just the data itself.
