## Executive Summary

The content emphasizes that understanding these concepts is not just about vocabulary, but about mastering the structured thinking required for successful consulting interviews and client engagements.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **MECE (Mutually Exclusive, Collectively Exhaustive):** A framework for ensuring that a problem is broken down without overlapping points or missing gaps. Interviewers prioritize this structural integrity.
- **Buy-In:** The process of gaining agreement from key stakeholders. A recommendation's value is dependent on the client's commitment to it.
- **Run Rate:** Extrapolating current financial performance to an annualized value (e.g., £100k/month = £1.2M run rate).
- **Benchmarking:** Evaluating a client's performance against industry peers or best-in-class competitors to identify performance gaps.
- **The "So-What?":** The critical practice of providing the implication behind every data point. Data without actionable insight is insufficient in consulting.
