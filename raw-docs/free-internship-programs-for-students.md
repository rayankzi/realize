# Free Internship Programs for Prestigious Companies

**Topic/Category:** Career Development / Internship Opportunities
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To introduce college students to two free organizations (SEO Career and Lime Connect) that facilitate internship placements at top-tier corporate companies.

---

## Executive Summary

This reel highlights two major free programs designed to help college students, including those with disabilities, secure prestigious summer internships at world-class companies like Google, Goldman Sachs, and J.P. Morgan. The creator provides a quick overview of SEO Career and Lime Connect, emphasizing their accessibility and the high-value partnerships they maintain with over 200 top corporations.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **SEO Career:** A free organization partnering with over 200 top companies to recruit and prepare students for corporate summer internships.
- **Lime Connect:** A free organization specifically for students with disabilities, offering specialized programs for freshmen and rising juniors.
- **Accessibility:** Both programs are completely free for students, with SEO Career accepting applications on a rolling basis.
- **Prestigious Network:** The programs provide direct paths to internships at major firms across finance, tech, and other sectors.
