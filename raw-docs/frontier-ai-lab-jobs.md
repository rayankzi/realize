# How to Get Hired at a Frontier AI Lab

**Topic/Category:** Career Advice / Machine Learning / AI Engineering
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To share alternative "on-ramps" for landing a job at top AI labs (OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepMind) without requiring a PhD or elite connections, specifically referencing a guide by Vladimir Feinberg.

## Executive Summary

This reel highlights a guide by Vladimir Feinberg, a lead researcher at Google DeepMind, on how to break into frontier AI labs. It debunks the myth that a PhD is the only path and identifies two specific technical niches—kernel work and agent research—as accessible entry points. The content provides a concrete learning path involving hands-on practice with the "Jax Scaling Book" and mentions a potential fast-track opportunity for those who complete the exercises.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Alternative Paths:** You don't necessarily need a PhD or high-level connections to work at OpenAI or Anthropic.
- **Niche 1: Kernel Work:** High-demand work focused on optimizing how models run on hardware (chips). It requires "physical hardware intuition" (e.g., understanding data bottlenecks), which is difficult for current AI tools to replicate.
- **Niche 2: Agent Research:** This involves designing controlled experiments to evaluate LLM agent behavior, rather than just using or prompting them.
- **Hands-on Learning:** Practical experience is valued over theoretical knowledge. The recommended path is to build models from scratch.
- **Direct Access:** Vladimir Feinberg offers to fast-track candidates who can prove they completed the exercises in the Jax Scaling Book.
