# How to Get Your Interviewer's Email Address

**Topic/Category:** Career Advice / Job Search Strategy
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To provide three actionable methods for obtaining an interviewer's contact information to send follow-up thank-you emails.

---

## Executive Summary

Landing a job often requires a follow-up thank-you email, but finding the specific email address of an interviewer can be a hurdle. This video breaks down three practical ways to find that information, highlighting a "pro-tip" method that not only gets you the email but also strengthens your relationship with the recruiter.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Method 1: Check Existing Invites.** Interviewer details are often hidden in calendar invites or previous email threads from the scheduling phase.
- **Method 2: Pattern Recognition.** Use the format of the recruiter's email (e.g., first.last@company.com) to guess the interviewer's address if direct info isn't found.
- **Method 3: Ask the Recruiter (Most Effective).** Directly asking the recruiter for the interviewer's contact info is the most reliable method. It shows proactive interest and keeps you visible in the recruiter's inbox.
- **Strategic Persistence:** Send the request to the recruiter immediately after the interview to ensure you can follow up within the optimal timeframe.
