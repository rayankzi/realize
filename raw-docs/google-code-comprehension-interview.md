## Executive Summary

Google has fundamentally shifted its software engineering interview process by introducing a "Code Comprehension" round. Instead of traditional algorithmic challenges (like LeetCode), candidates are now tasked with fixing bugs in a broken codebase using an AI assistant. This change emphasizes AI fluency and real-world debugging skills over rote memorization of algorithms. The creator highlights that existing platforms are no longer sufficient and promotes VeloCode.ai as a solution for practicing this specific type of interview.

- **Shift to Real-World Skills:** Google is moving away from testing pure coding ability in a vacuum toward testing how well developers can navigate and fix existing, broken codebases.

- **LeetCode Obsolescence:** The creator argues that traditional "LeetCode-style" preparation is no longer enough to land jobs at top tech companies like Google due to this shift.
- **New Standard for Prep:** "AI fluency" is described as the new bar for software engineering candidates.
