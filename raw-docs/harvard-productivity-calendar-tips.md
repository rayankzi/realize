# Harvard Student's Guide to Balancing 4 Jobs & a Social Life

**Topic/Category:** Productivity & Time Management
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To share a comprehensive system for organizing a high-achieving student's schedule using Google Calendar, the Four Quadrant method, and time blocking.

---

## Executive Summary

The creator, a Harvard student managing four jobs (author, non-profit leader, board member, and content creator), breaks down her productivity system. She emphasizes planning ahead based on priorities, using a structured goal-setting framework, and implementing a color-coded Google Calendar to maintain balance across personal, academic, and professional domains.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **The Four Quadrant Method:** Categorize life into Personal, Academics, Career, and Relationships. Choose one area to prioritize each semester and ensure the schedule reflects this intention.
- **Strategic Scheduling:** Align class times with lifestyle needs. The creator avoided Friday and Monday morning classes to facilitate her frequent weekend travel for keynote speeches.
- **Time Blocking & Stacking:** Group similar activities together. For example, stacking all academic work on specific days and career-related meetings on others to minimize context switching.
- **Visual Categorization:** Use specific colors in Google Calendar to "mentally catalogue" different life areas, making a complex schedule easier to navigate.

## Narrative & Step-by-Step Breakdown

1. **Pre-Semester Planning:** Before the semester starts, sit down to define priorities using the Four Quadrant framework.
2. **Intentional Goal Setting:** Set specific intentions for each quadrant and ensure the schedule (especially class selection) supports them.
3. **Daily Organization:**
    - **Mondays/Wednesdays:** Heavy on meetings (9 AM - 1 PM) and career-related work.
    - **Time Blocks:** Dedicate specific days to "stacks" of work (e.g., all-day academics).
4. **Calendar Maintenance:** Use the Google Calendar "Tasks" feature with checkboxes to manage daily to-dos within the visual schedule.
