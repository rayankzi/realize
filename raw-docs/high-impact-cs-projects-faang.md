# High-Impact CS Projects for FAANG Interviews

**Topic/Category:** Career Development / Computer Science
**Primary Goal of the Post:** To provide computer science students with unique and high-impact project ideas that stand out to recruiters and help secure interviews at top tech companies, moving away from generic projects.

---

## Executive Summary

This carousel, created by a former FAANG intern (who accepted an offer at NVIDIA), highlights six specific and ambitious project categories that go beyond the typical "todo list" or "weather app" found on most CS resumes. By focusing on advanced fields like Reinforcement Learning, Generative AI, and real-world utility tools like Chrome extensions or data dashboards, students can demonstrate a higher level of technical competence and practical problem-solving skills that are highly valued by top-tier tech companies.

## Key Insights & Main Ideas

- **Move Beyond the Basics:** Generic projects don't differentiate you. Aim for projects that involve complex logic or solve specific, niche problems.
- **Leverage Modern AI:** Incorporate Reinforcement Learning and Generative AI (using tools like LangGraph or Claude AI) to show you are at the forefront of tech trends.
- **Focus on Utility and Users:** Building tools that people actually use (like Chrome extensions for students) or dashboards that visualize meaningful data shows a product-oriented mindset.
- **End-to-End Development:** Don't just build a demo; build a complete project with a full stack, real users, and deployment.
- **Specific Knowledge:** Training an AI for a "weirdly specific" task (like boxing) shows deep dive capability and passion.
