# The High Performer Question: A Simple Interview Strategy to Secure Job Offers

**Topic/Category:** Career Advice / Interview Strategy
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To teach a specific, powerful question candidates should ask interviewers to understand exactly what the company is looking for and position themselves as the ideal candidate.

---

## Executive Summary

The video highlights a game-changing interview tactic called the "High Performer Question." By asking interviewers what a "high performer" looks like in the role, candidates force the employer to reveal their ideal criteria. This allows the candidate to immediately tailor their skills and responses to match that specific profile, effectively using the interviewer's own words to prove they are the best fit for the position.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **The "High Performer Question":** "You're probably going to interview a lot of people who can do the job. But in your opinion, what does the high performer look like?"
- **Information Discovery:** This question compels interviewers to list the specific qualities, daily actions, and "above and beyond" behaviors they value most.
- **Strategic Mirroring:** Once the interviewer defines the high performer, your goal is to "meld" your existing skills, knowledge, and attributes to that specific description.
- **Interview as Strategy:** Interviewing is likened to a "chess game" where getting the employer to define success gives you the winning move.
- **Simplicity is Key:** The strategy is simple to execute—ask the question and then "stop talking" to let the interviewer provide the roadmap.
