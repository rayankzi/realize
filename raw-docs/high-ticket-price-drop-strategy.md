# How to Price Drop Like a Top 1% Sales Closer

**Topic/Category:** Sales & High-Ticket Closing
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To debunk the common "silence after price drop" advice and provide a structured framework for dropping prices that maintains control and leads to higher conversion.

---

## Executive Summary

This reel features a high-ticket sales expert critiquing a common piece of advice: "state the price and then stay silent." The creator argues that this is the "worst sales advice" and instead advocates for "leading the conversation." By using psychological anchoring, framing the cost as an "investment," and guiding the prospect toward the best-fit solution rather than offering a confusing menu of options, closers can maintain authority and reduce friction during the most sensitive part of the sales call.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Silence is NOT Golden:** The old adage that "the first person to talk after the price drop loses" is debunked as "bullshit." Silence gives away control; leading the conversation maintains it.
- **Language Matters:** Never use the words "cost" or "price." Always use the word **"investment."**
- **The Power of Anchoring:** Mention a higher-priced tier first ($20,000) that the prospect likely doesn't need to make the actual price ($5,000) seem significantly more affordable.
- **Payment Plan Framing:** Break down large investments into monthly payments (e.g., "$450 a month") and emphasize the ROI (Return on Investment) timeline (e.g., "within the first 90 days").
- **Prescriptive Selling:** Don't ask the prospect which package they want. Instead, tell them which one is the best fit for them and explain why. This prevents "analysis paralysis" and keeps the closer in the expert role.

## Narrative & Step-by-Step Breakdown

The video follows a "Reaction" format where the speaker analyzes a clip of a woman (Ava) performing a sales call.

1.  **The Hook:** The video starts with a clip of Ava saying "The cost is going to be $5,000" followed by the speaker immediately calling out the "stay silent" advice as a major mistake.
2.  **Step 1: Get Permission:** Ask the prospect if they are ready to hear the investment details.
    - *Script:* "Hey John, are you cool if I run through exactly what the investment is going to look like?"
3.  **Step 2: The Price Drop (The "1% Closer" Method):**
    - Anchor with a high tier ($20k).
    - Position the prospect for the lower tier ($5k).
    - Break it down into monthly payments.
    - Validate with ROI potential.
4.  **Step 3: The Check-in:** Ask an open-ended question to lead the conversation.
    - *Script:* "How does that fit for you?"
5.  **Critique of "Options":** The speaker explains why giving a prospect 3 options ($5k, $10k, $15k) and asking "which one would you like?" is a mistake—it gives the lead full control and they will usually just pick the cheapest.
6.  **The Solution:** Use "The Prescriptive Close."
    - *Script:* "Out of the different options we have, what I believe will be most beneficial for you is [Option A], and here's why..."

## Verbatim Templates & Scripts

### The Price Drop Script
> "Hey [Name], are you cool if I run through exactly what the investment is going to look like? So [Name], we've got two different tiers. We have our $20,000 package, which you're not going to need that much support. What I'm thinking for you is going to be our lower tier. The investment for the lower tier is only $5,000 and we can break that up to about $450 a month over the year. Most of our clients are getting that ROI within the first 90 days alone. So the investment is a no-brainer. But you let me know your thoughts. How does that fit for you?"

### The Prescriptive Close Script
> "Out of the different options we have, what I believe will be most beneficial for you is this [specific package]. And here's why. The investment is this [amount]. There's different ways we can take care of it. How would you like to go about it?"
