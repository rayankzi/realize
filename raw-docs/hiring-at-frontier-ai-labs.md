# How to Land a Job at a Frontier AI Lab

**Topic/Category:** AI Career Development / Machine Learning Engineering
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To share unconventional "on-ramps" for engineers and researchers to get hired at top AI labs like OpenAI, Anthropic, and DeepMind, specifically highlighting advice from a DeepMind researcher.

## Executive Summary

Vladimir Feinberg, the researcher leading Gemini pre-training at Google DeepMind, published a guide debunking the myth that only PhDs from elite universities can land roles at frontier AI labs. He identifies two specific technical niches—kernel optimization and rigorous agent research—that are in high demand and offers a personal fast-track for anyone who can prove they have mastered the fundamentals through hands-on practice.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Beyond the PhD Myth:** While many AI researchers hold PhDs, it is not the only path. Specific technical expertise can serve as a powerful alternative entry point.
- **On-Ramp 1: Kernel Work:** This involves writing code that controls how models run on chips. It requires "hardware intuition" and understanding data bottlenecks, a skill set that AI coding tools currently lack.
- **On-Ramp 2: Agent Research:** Focuses on setting up controlled experiments to evaluate how LLM agents behave, rather than just using or prompting them.
- **The "Fast-Track" Offer:** Vladimir Feinberg has offered to personally fast-track candidates who can prove they have completed every exercise in the *Jax Scaling Book* and built a model from scratch.

## Narrative & Step-by-Step Breakdown

- **Beginning:** Introduction to Vladimir Feinberg and his guide on getting hired at frontier labs. Acknowledgement of the common assumption that only advanced ML PhDs or those with deep connections get in.
- **Middle:** Detailed breakdown of the two identified "on-ramps":
    1. **Kernel Work:** Deep dives into hardware efficiency and chip-level code.
    2. **Agent Research:** Moving from "prompting" to rigorous experimental evaluation.
- **End:** Practical starting point recommendation: Working through the *Jax Scaling Book*. Emphasis on doing every exercise by hand and building a model from scratch to earn a potential fast-track referral.
