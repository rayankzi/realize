# How to Stand Out When Applying for Startup Jobs

**Topic/Category:** Career Advice / Startup Hiring
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To provide actionable tips for job seekers on how to stand out and avoid common pitfalls when reaching out to startup founders and hiring managers.

---

## Executive Summary

The creator discusses the overwhelming response to her startup's internship roles (over 1,000 applications) and uses the opportunity to share why most candidates fail to stand out. She emphasizes that personal branding, attention to detail, and proactive research are more important than traditional metrics like GPA when applying for roles in dynamic environments like startups or marketing.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Attention to Detail is Non-Negotiable:** Spelling the recipient's name correctly is a basic filter. Failure to do so leads to immediate rejection.
- **Portfolios Over GPAs:** For marketing and creative roles, tangible proof of work (a portfolio) is far more valuable than academic scores.
- **Proactive Research:** Even if a new project is secret, researching a founder's previous public work shows initiative and genuine interest.
- **Social Media as a Resume:** When reaching out via platforms like Instagram, your profile (especially your profile picture) serves as a first impression and a reflection of your branding skills.
- **Invitational Branding:** A profile picture should be clear, friendly, and "inviting," making the recipient want to open the message.
