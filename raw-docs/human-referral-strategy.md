# The Human Way to Get Referrals

**Topic/Category:** Career Development / Job Search Strategy
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To teach viewers a specific, low-friction messaging strategy for getting referrals at top companies like Microsoft by focusing on building a relationship rather than asking for a favor immediately.

---

## Executive Summary

Many job seekers fail to get referrals because their cold outreach is too formal, too long, or starts with an immediate ask of a stranger. This reel breaks down the "human-first" approach: leading with genuine interest in the person's work and asking for a brief conversation instead of a referral. By treating the outreach as a networking opportunity rather than a transaction, the creator landed a referral at Microsoft and suggests that most people will naturally offer a referral after a good 15-minute chat.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Humanity Over Formality:** Stop using stiff phrases like "I hope this finds you well." Being "human" is more important than being "qualified" in cold outreach.
- **Stop the "Cold Ask":** Asking for a referral from a stranger before a relationship exists is the fastest way to get ignored. Nobody owes a stranger a favor.
- **Interest-Led Outreach:** Start by complimenting a specific piece of their work that genuinely impressed you.
- **The 15-Minute Rule:** Ask for a short 15-minute "brain pick" session rather than a referral.
- **Lower the Barrier:** Give them an "out" by saying you understand if they are busy and would appreciate even a text-based tip.
- **The "Offer" Effect:** If the conversation goes well, most people will offer to refer you without you even having to ask.

### The "Microsoft Referral" Cold Message Template

> Hey [Name], your work on [specific thing] is something I keep coming back to — genuinely one of the most impressive things I have seen in this space.
> 
> I am in the process of applying to Microsoft and would love 15 minutes to pick your brain. Totally understand if life is busy — even a tip or two over text would be incredibly valuable to me.
