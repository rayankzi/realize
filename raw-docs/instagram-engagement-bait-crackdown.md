# Instagram's Crackdown on "Engagement Bait" Reels

**Topic/Category:** Social Media Marketing / Instagram Strategy
**Primary Goal of the Post:** To inform creators about Instagram's new policy of penalizing Reels that use "engagement bait" tactics (like fast-moving text) and provide tips for creating high-quality text-based Reels that align with the platform's goals.

---

## Executive Summary

Instagram is actively moving away from rewarding "shady tactics" that artificially boost watch time, specifically targeting Reels where text moves too fast to read, forcing rewatches. According to Head of Instagram Adam Mosseri, the platform is proactively identifying and penalizing this "engagement bait." The post emphasizes that while these tricks once worked, they now frustrate audiences and hurt reach. The shift is towards content that provides genuine value and enjoyment, rather than relying on loopholes in the algorithm.

## Key Insights & Main Ideas

- **The Crackdown on "Engagement Bait":** Instagram is penalizing short videos that trick users into rewatching by making on-screen text disappear too quickly.
- **Algorithm vs. User Experience:** While artificial watch time used to boost reach, Instagram now prioritizes a positive user experience. Frustrating your audience leads to skips and lower engagement.
- **"Read the Caption" Reels:** Short Reels with no value other than a hook that forces the video to loop while the user reads the caption are also being "left behind" by the algorithm and ignored by users.
- **Content Integrity:** Good content should work on its own. If you need "tricks" to get views, the content itself isn't doing its job.
- **Future of Text-Based Reels:** Text-based Reels are still viable, but they must be executed with transparency and value-first principles.

## Detailed Breakdown / Step-by-Step

### The Current State of the Algorithm
- **Slide 1:** Hook - Instagram is penalizing a specific type of Reel lately (based on official info).
- **Slide 2:** Source - Adam Mosseri (Head of Instagram) confirmed they are looking for videos that "fool a user" into rewatching to read text.
- **Slide 3:** Definition - These are Reels where text moves too fast, "forcing" replays or pauses.
- **Slide 4:** The Label - This is officially called **Engagement Bait**.
- **Slide 5:** The Reality - These Reels used to perform well due to watch time metrics, but Instagram is "done with it."
- **Slide 6-8:** User Sentiment - Even if the algorithm hasn't flagged a post yet, users are skipping this type of content because they are tired of the frustration.

### How to Use Text-Based Reels Correctly
- **Slide 9:** Transition - How to keep using text-based Reels without hurting reach.
- **Slide 10:** **5 Things to Keep in Mind:**
    1. **Message:** Ensure the Reel delivers the core message on its own; the caption should be a "bonus," not a requirement.
    2. **Length:** Give people enough time to read. Don't flash text too quickly just for replays.
    3. **Audio:** Choose audio that supports the specific message of the Reel.
    4. **Clips:** Add personality by including yourself in the video rather than overusing stock footage or nature shots.
    5. **Text:** Ensure high contrast between text and background to prevent users from scrolling past due to poor readability.
