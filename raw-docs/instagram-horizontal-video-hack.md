# How to Post Horizontal Videos on Instagram Without Black Borders

**Topic/Category:** Social Media Tutorial / Instagram Hacks
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To teach users how to upload horizontal (4:3) videos to their Instagram feed without the app automatically adding black letterboxing borders.

---

## Executive Summary

This tutorial addresses a common frustration for Instagram users: the automatic addition of black borders when posting horizontal content. The creator demonstrates that the key to maintaining a clean horizontal look on the main feed is to avoid using Instagram's native music feature during the upload process. By adding music in external editing software like CapCut first and posting as a standard "Post" rather than a "Reel" (which Instagram forces if you use its native tools), the video remains horizontal without the distracting black bars.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **The "Native Music" Trap:** Adding music directly within the Instagram app automatically converts a horizontal video into a vertical "Reel," which forces the black border to fill the 9:16 space.
- **External Editing is Key:** To maintain a horizontal aspect ratio, you must finalize your video (including music) in an external editor like CapCut before uploading.
- **"Post" vs. "Reel" Tab:** Use the "Post" tab for the upload to ensure the horizontal format is respected on the feed, even if it appears as a Reel in the dedicated Reels tab.
- **Feed vs. Reels Tab Behavior:** Horizontal videos will still show bars in the 9:16 Reels tab, but will appear correctly (horizontally) in the main scrollable feed.

## Narrative & Step-by-Step Breakdown

1.  **Preparation:** Edit your video in an external app (e.g., CapCut). Ensure all music and audio are finalized there.
2.  **Start Upload:** Open Instagram and navigate to the **Post** tab (not the Reels tab).
3.  **Selection:** Select your horizontal (e.g., 4:3) video and hit **Next**.
4.  **The Critical Step:** In the editing screen, **do not add music** or use Instagram's native editing tools.
5.  **Finalize:** Select or upload a cover image, write your caption, and post as normal.
6.  **Verification:** Check your main feed to see the horizontal video without the black borders.
