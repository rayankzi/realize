# 3 Instagram Updates That Are Changing How You Post in 2026

**Topic/Category:** Social Media Marketing / Instagram Strategy
**Primary Goal of the Post:** To inform creators and businesses about three new Instagram features — carousel reordering, clickable reels, and Instagram Instants — and provide actionable next steps for leveraging each one.

---

## Executive Summary

Reach Media highlights three recent Instagram feature updates that shift how creators manage and distribute content. The ability to reorder carousel slides after posting lets you optimize based on performance data. Clickable reels turn passive video views into direct navigation to your other content or profile. Instagram Instants introduces ephemeral, unfiltered photo/video sharing within DMs, signaling a platform push toward raw, authentic connection. Together, these updates reward creators who are willing to iterate, interlink, and get more personal with their audience.

## Key Insights & Main Ideas

- **Insight 1:** Carousel content is no longer static — you can rearrange slides post-publication to front-load your best-performing or most-saved slides, effectively A/B testing your carousel order over time.
- **Insight 2:** Reels can now contain clickable links to other reels or your profile, eliminating the friction of "link in bio" and turning every reel into a navigation hub for your content ecosystem.
- **Insight 3:** Instagram Instants is a new DM-based feature for sending quick, unedited photos or videos to mutual followers that disappear after one view — pushing creators toward real-time, lo-fi connection rather than polished content.

## Detailed Breakdown / Step-by-Step

**Slide 1:** Title card — "Instagram updates you should know about..." with Reach Media branding on a deep maroon background. Sets the hook.

**Slide 2: Reorder Carousels After Posting**
- *What's New:* Instagram is testing the ability to reorder carousel slides after posting, allowing rearrangement based on performance.
- *The Impact:* Content is no longer fixed. You can improve performance after posting.
- *What To Do Next:*
  1. Check your last 3 carousels
  2. Find your most saved/shared slide
  3. Move that to the front
  4. Test different slide orders

**Slide 3: Clickable Reels**
- *What's New:* You can add clickable links inside reels, linking to other reels or your profile.
- *The Impact:* Reels now drive action, not just reach. No more relying solely on "link in bio."
- *How To Set Up:*
  1. Open Edits and start a new reel
  2. Add your main video or background clip
  3. Click Links
  4. Select Reels
  5. Choose My Reels and add the reels you want to feature
  6. Position them on screen like mini clickable reels
  7. Add text telling people what to tap

**Slide 4: Instagram Instants**
- *What's New:* A feature for sending quick, unfiltered photos/videos inside DMs to mutual followers — viewed once, then disappears.
- *The Impact:* A shift toward real-time, unfiltered content. No need for perfect lighting, polished edits, or curated content.
- *What To Do Next:*
  1. Start sharing more behind-the-scenes moments
  2. Focus on connection over perfection
  3. Use this for "close friends" style content
  4. Think: how can I feel more human, not more polished?
