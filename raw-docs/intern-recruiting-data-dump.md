# 182 Applications, 3 Offers: What My Intern Recruiting Data Actually Showed

**Topic/Category:** CS Career Advice / Tech Internship Recruiting
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** Share real, data-backed insights from a full tech internship recruiting season to help CS students optimize their job search strategy.

---

## Executive Summary

The creator tracked their entire tech internship recruiting pipeline — 182 applications, 64 online assessments (OAs), 25 interviews, and 3 offers — and distilled the data into actionable takeaways. The most counterintuitive finding: cold applications significantly outperformed referrals in OA conversion (70% vs. 16%), meaning speed of application matters far more than waiting for a referral. The typical timeline spanned about 3 months (August through December), though outliers like Nvidia offers came as late as March of the following year.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Speed beats referrals:** Cold applications converted to OAs at 70%, while referrals only converted at 16%. Applying the day a role opens — using tools like the Simplified Jobs repo — was the single biggest lever.
- **Referrals are additive, not a prerequisite:** Get a referral if you can and tag it on, but don't wait for one. Apply first, add the referral after.
- **The timeline is longer than you think:** Most recruiting happens in a 3-month window, but companies can reject or ghost you a full year later. Don't interpret silence as rejection too early.
- **Early career / insight programs are underrated:** These fast-track programs can get you visibility much earlier in the process. The creator plans a dedicated video on this.
- **Rejection is redirection:** With a 1.6% offer rate from applications, high rejection volume is normal and expected — not a signal to stop.
