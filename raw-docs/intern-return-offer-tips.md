# How to Not Fumble Your Return Offer: The "Dumb Question" Framework

**Topic/Category:** Career Advice / Internship Strategy
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To teach interns how to ask questions effectively to avoid being perceived as incompetent and to secure a return offer.

## Executive Summary

This reel addresses the common misconception that "there are no dumb questions," arguing that for interns, poorly timed or researched questions can jeopardize their return offer prospects. The creator provides a specific hierarchy for seeking help and emphasizes the importance of demonstrating effort before escalating questions to mentors or managers.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Dumb Questions Exist:** Asking questions without prior research or repeating questions already answered can signal a lack of initiative or poor listening skills.
- **The Hierarchy of Asking:** Always follow a specific order when seeking help: self-research -> peers -> junior teammates -> senior leaders.
- **Show Your Work:** When escalating a question, always lead with what you have already tried to solve the problem.
- **Personal Knowledge Management:** Keeping a log of unfamiliar terms and meeting notes is crucial for avoiding repetitive questions.
