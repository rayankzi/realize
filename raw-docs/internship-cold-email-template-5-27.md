# The Ultimate Internship Cold Email Template

**Topic/Category:** Career Development / Networking
**Primary Goal of the Post:** To provide a proven cold email template for students to secure dream internships and opportunities by directly reaching out to professionals.

---

## Executive Summary

This post emphasizes the power of proactive networking through cold emailing. It features a "10x intern and chronic cold emailer" sharing a highly effective template designed to grab attention and demonstrate value. The template focuses on aligning personal aspirations with a company's mission while highlighting specific accomplishments and a unique "edge" as a young professional.

## Key Insights & Main Ideas

- **Proactivity Wins:** The core message is that you don't have to wait for job postings; you can "just send a cold email" to anyone you want to work with.
- **Mission Alignment:** The template starts by explicitly connecting the sender's interests with the company's specific mission or "obsession," showing deep research and genuine interest.
- **Value-First Approach:** Instead of just asking for a job, the template highlights what the sender brings to the table: relevant experience, specific metrics-based accomplishments, and a unique professional "edge."
- **Humility and Support:** Expressing a willingness to take on any task ("no tasks would be too big or too small") signals a strong work ethic and a desire to truly support the team's work.

## Verbatim Templates & Scripts

> **Subject: Ready to work - student with XYZ experience**
>
> Hi XYZ,
>
> My name is XYZ. I’m an aspiring XYZ and (major) student at XYZ University. XYZ Company’s obsession with solving the problem of XYZ with XYZ is exactly the kind of mission I want to be a part of. What you need to know about me:
>
> - I’ve worked in (industry/role) across (company/type of orgs), focusing on (relevant function).
> - I recently (specific accomplishment with metric or outcome).
> - My edge as a young professional is (specific skill: execution, research, ops, storytelling, analysis, etc.) I want to bring this to XYZ company by doing (specific idea).
>
> I want to intern for XYZ. No tasks would be too big or too small for me. I just want to support your work. Are there any opportunities available for this (season)?
>
> I’ve attached my resume for your reference.
>
> Gratefully,
> XYZ
