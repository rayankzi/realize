# The Chronic Cold Emailer's Dream Internship Template

**Topic/Category:** Career Development / Networking
**Primary Goal of the Post:** To provide a proven cold email template that students can use to land "dream internships" and professional opportunities.

---

## Executive Summary

This post, created by a self-described "10x intern and chronic cold emailer," highlights the power of direct outreach. It provides a structured, fill-in-the-blanks cold email template designed to show passion for a company's mission while highlighting the applicant's specific value proposition and "edge" as a young professional.

## Key Insights & Main Ideas

- **Direct Action:** You can (and should) send cold emails to anyone you want to work for.
- **Mission Alignment:** The template emphasizes starting with why you align with the company's specific "obsession" or mission.
- **Value-First Approach:** Instead of just asking for a job, the template focuses on what the applicant has done (metrics/outcomes) and their specific "edge."
- **Low Friction:** The template is designed to be copy-pasted and filled with personal details, making the process efficient for the sender.

## Detailed Breakdown / Step-by-Step

### Slide-by-Slide Summary
- **Slide 1:** A "friendly reminder" that cold emailing is a valid and effective strategy, featuring the creator (a "10x intern").

### Cold Email Structure Breakdown
1. **Subject Line:** Clear and professional ("Ready to work - student with XYZ experience").
2. **Introduction:** Name, current role/major, and university.
3. **Mission Hook:** Specifically mention the company's mission and why it resonates with you.
4. **The "What You Need to Know":** Three bullet points highlighting industry experience, a specific accomplishment with a metric, and your unique "edge" (e.g., storytelling, analysis).
5. **The Ask:** Explicitly stating the desire to intern and asking about availability for a specific season.
6. **Closing:** Attachment of resume and a professional sign-off ("Gratefully").

## Verbatim Templates & Scripts

> **Subject:** Ready to work - student with XYZ experience
>
> Hi XYZ,
>
> My name is XYZ. I'm an aspiring XYZ and (major) student at XYZ University. XYZ Company's obsession with solving the problem of XYZ with XYZ is exactly the kind of mission I want to be a part of. What you need to know about me:
>
> * I've worked in (industry/role) across (company/type of orgs), focusing on (relevant function).
> * I recently (specific accomplishment with metric or outcome).
> * My edge as a young professional is (specific skill: execution, research, ops, storytelling, analysis, etc.) I want to bring this to XYZ company by doing (specific idea).
>
> I want to intern for XYZ. No tasks would be too big or too small for me. I just want to support your work. Are there any opportunities available for this (season)?
>
> I've attached my resume for your reference.
>
> Gratefully,
> XYZ
