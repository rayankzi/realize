# Best Tools for Landing an Internship

**Topic/Category:** Career / Internship Prep / Job Search
**Primary Goal of the Post:** To share four essential, free or accessible tools that help students streamline the internship application process — from auto-filling applications to tracking outcomes.

---

## Executive Summary

This 6-slide carousel by a student/early-career creator breaks down the best tools to use when hunting for internships. The creator walks through four specific, actionable resources: Simplify (auto-fill Chrome extension), Overleaf's Jake Resume Template, LinkedIn (strictly for networking, not applications), and a free application tracker spreadsheet. The final slide drives engagement with a comment-gated resource guide drop, and the caption reinforces the hype with an encouraging "You got this!!"

## Key Insights & Main Ideas

- **Simplify saves serious application time:** It's a Chrome extension that auto-fills your personal information across job application forms, dramatically reducing friction when submitting at volume.
- **Jake's Resume Template on Overleaf is industry-standard:** Clean, one-page, LaTeX-based format that is widely recognized in the tech industry — recruiters are very familiar with it.
- **LinkedIn is for networking, not job applications:** The creator explicitly advises against using LinkedIn to apply to jobs, and instead recommends using it to reach out directly to recruiters, employees, and alumni.
- **An application tracker spreadsheet is essential:** Keeping a free spreadsheet to log where you applied, interview stages, and final outcomes helps stay organized across dozens of applications.
- **Comment-gating drives engagement:** The creator uses a "Comment INTERN" mechanic to unlock a full resource guide, encouraging comments which boost algorithmic reach.
