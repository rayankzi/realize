# Internship Reflection & Career Growth Strategy

**Topic/Category:** Career Advice / Software Engineering
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To share reflections on completing a Vercel internship and provide advice on how to maximize learnings during an internship through journaling.

---

## Executive Summary

The creator reflects on their growth during a four-month internship at Vercel, emphasizing the value of full-time level autonomy. As they transition to a vacation in Beijing before starting a new internship at Databricks, they offer a key piece of advice for other interns: maintain both public and private journals to track work, personal feelings, and career preferences.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Embrace Autonomy:** Being treated like a full-time engineer can be uncomfortable but is a major catalyst for growth.
- **Dual Journaling System:**
    - **Public Journal:** Shared with the team/company to document weekly work and accomplishments.
    - **Private Journal:** For personal reflections on feelings, learnings, and career fit.
- **Continuous Reflection:** Internships are prime opportunities to identify what types of work and company cultures align with your goals.
- **The "Intern Event" Close:** Use final intern events to bond with peers before departing.
