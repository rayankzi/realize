# Maximizing Weeks 2 and 3 of Your Internship or New Job

**Topic/Category:** Career Development / Internship Advice
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To guide new interns and employees on how to navigate the critical transition from onboarding (Week 1) to actual work (Weeks 2-3) to ensure a return offer.

---

## Executive Summary

Weeks 2 and 3 are pivotal because they transition from general orientation to specific department integration. This period sets the tone for the entire internship. By focusing on building deep team connections and delivering high-quality work immediately, individuals can position themselves for higher-level projects and, ultimately, a return offer.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **The Transition:** Week 2 moves you from general company orientation to your specific department or group.
- **Two Tiers of Work:** You will be assigned both long-term projects (capstone/priority) and short-term projects (housekeeping/recurring).
- **Function-Specific Training:** Expect deep dives into specific systems and job functions (e.g., mock calls for sales roles).
- **The "High-Level" Pipeline:** Early proof of reliability leads to more important assignments sooner.
- **Foundation for Return Offers:** The reputation built in these two weeks often determines whether you are remembered as a "good hire."

## Narrative & Step-by-Step Breakdown

### The Roadmap: Weeks 2 and 3

1.  **Department Integration:** You are split into your specific working group for the duration of your role.
2.  **Extended Onboarding:** Continued introductions and team-specific orientations.
3.  **Project Assignment:** Receiving both "capstone" style long-term work and "housekeeping" short-term tasks.
4.  **Skills Training:** Practical training on job-specific systems and best practices.
5.  **Performance Baseline:** Establishing your reputation for dependability and quality.

### Two Primary Goals

-   **Goal 1: Immediate Team Connection:** Go beyond surface-level introductions; aim for "real connections" to facilitate cross-department networking.
-   **Goal 2: Quality Work Output:** Produce exceptional work on even the smallest tasks to build trust for higher-stakes projects.
