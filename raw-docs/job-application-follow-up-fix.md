# Stop Getting Ghosted: The 30-Second Follow-Up Fix

**Topic/Category:** Career Advice / Job Search Strategy
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To explain the importance of following up after a job application and provide a structured template for doing so to increase interview chances.

---

## Executive Summary

Many qualified candidates are ghosted because they stop after submitting their application and referral. This reel explains that the real work begins *after* clicking submit. By following up with multiple connections at the company using a specific 5-point structure, candidates can transform their application from a single "shot in the dark" into a multi-person advocacy campaign, significantly increasing their chances of securing a first-round interview.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **The Common Mistake:** Most applicants stop once they've had coffee chats, a referral, and clicked submit.
- **The Solution:** Immediate follow-up with all internal connections after submitting the application.
- **Leveraging Relationships:** Moving from one application to 5-7 internal advocates.
- **Hiring Reality:** Many roles are filled before postings go live, and internal vouching is often the deciding factor for moving a candidate forward.

## Narrative & Step-by-Step Breakdown

- **Beginning (The Hook):** Addresses the frustration of being qualified yet never hearing back from applications.
- **Middle (The Fix):** Outlines the 5-step follow-up structure to use immediately after applying.
- **End (The Conclusion):** Emphasizes that the difference between getting an interview and being ghosted is the follow-up.

## Verbatim Templates & Scripts

### Post-Application Follow-Up Structure

> 1. **Open with a quick greeting.**
> 2. **Confirm you submitted the application.**
> 3. **Reshare your resume.**
> 4. **Ask for a referral.**
> 5. **Close with appreciation and next steps.**
