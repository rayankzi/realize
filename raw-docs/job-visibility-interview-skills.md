# How to Get Seen and Chosen: Two Skills Most Job Seekers Ignore

**Topic/Category:** Career Development / Job Search Strategy
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To shift the viewer's focus from mass applications to personal branding and interview preparation as the primary drivers of job offers.

---

## Executive Summary

The creator argues that landing job offers isn't about being the most impressive candidate, but about mastering two specific skills: personal branding (visibility) and interviewing. By moving away from competitive application pools and focusing on being searchable by recruiters, and then treating the interview as a rehearsable sales pitch, job seekers can join the "10% of people getting 90% of the offers."

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Stop Competing in Applications:** Direct applications are high-competition and low-reward. Instead, compete in personal branding and visibility.
- **Show Up Where Opportunities Are:** Recruiters are actively looking for candidates. You need to be visible in LinkedIn feeds, recruiter searches, referrals, and through agencies.
- **Searchability is an Advantage:** Simply being easily searchable gives you a massive advantage over peers who only apply through portals.
- **Interviewing is a Sales Skill:** By the time you reach the interview stage, you are already qualified. The job goes to the candidate who "sells" their qualifications most effectively.
- **Interviewing is Rehearsable:** Interviewing is not an innate talent but a skill that can be dramatically improved through a week of focused practice.
