## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Automation Saves Time:** Dedicating a consistent block of time (1-2 hours) daily and using tools like Simplify can significantly increase application volume without burnout.
- **Speed is a Competitive Advantage:** Applying within 24 hours of a job posting is critical for visibility.
- **Experience Over GPA:** Recruiters prioritize projects and work experience over academic grades (GPA).
- **Interviewing is a Skill:** Technical and behavioral interviews require intense practice and cannot be "winged."
- **Strategic Course Loading:** Taking courses during co-op/internship terms can free up valuable time in the final semesters for full-time job hunting.

- **Beginning:** The creator introduces the challenge of studying for exams while applying for jobs and states they landed big tech interviews without referrals through time management.
- **The Five Tricks:**

    3. **Apply Early:** Apply to job postings that are less than 24 hours old.
    4. **Prioritize Projects:** Focus on building relevant projects rather than chasing a perfect GPA.
