# Skip the CV: The Secret to Landing Internships via LinkedIn DMs

**Topic/Category:** Career Development / Job Searching
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To teach viewers how to bypass traditional application systems by using a specific LinkedIn DM structure to land internships.

---

## Executive Summary

The creator shares a high-success strategy for landing internships by skipping the traditional CV-screening process. Instead of applying through portals where applications are often filtered by AI, the video advocates for direct outreach via LinkedIn DMs. By using a specific message structure that respects the recipient's time and offers immediate value, the creator claims to have landed two internships and multiple offers without ever sending a CV.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Bypass AI Filters:** Applying through traditional portals puts you at the bottom of a massive list screened by AI. Direct DMs skip this entirely.
- **Respect Recipient's Time:** Using phrases like "super quickly" signals that you aren't going to waste their time.
- **Low-Friction Introduction:** Acknowledge the cold nature of the message and provide a single, compelling "proof point" to trigger curiosity.
- **Value-First Approach:** Frame your availability as a desire to be "useful" rather than asking for a job or mentorship.
- **Consistency is Key:** Spending just 20 minutes a day on this strategy can yield responses within a week.

## Narrative & Step-by-Step Breakdown

1. **The Hook:** The creator starts with a bold claim: landing two internships and turning down nine offers without a CV.
2. **The Problem:** Traditional applications are a "thousand CVs" deep and screened by AI.
3. **The Solution:** LinkedIn DMs.
4. **The Message Structure:**
   - **Step 1: The Opener.** Use their name and "super quickly."
   - **Step 2: Acknowledgment & Identity.** Admit it's a cold message, say who you are, and drop one "proof point."
   - **Step 3: Availability & Value.** State your availability for the next few months and offer to be "useful."
   - **Step 4: The Ask.** A soft ask for a 15-minute intro call.
5. **The Guarantee:** Consistency (20 mins/day) leads to results.
