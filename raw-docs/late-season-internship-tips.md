# 7 Ways to Land a Late-Season Internship

**Topic/Category:** Career Development / Tech Internships
**Primary Goal of the Post:** To provide unconventional strategies for students to secure software engineering internships late in the recruiting cycle, focusing on startups and direct outreach.

---

## Executive Summary

This post outlines actionable, "hidden" strategies for landing a technical internship even when the traditional recruiting season seems over. The author, who landed 11 offers in 6 months, emphasizes moving away from saturated platforms like LinkedIn and instead focusing on early-stage startups, direct founder outreach, and building evidence-based projects using AI.

## Key Insights & Main Ideas

- **Avoid High-Competition Platforms:** Roles on LinkedIn are seen by hundreds instantly. Startups are a better target for "creating" your own role.
- **Direct Outreach Over HR:** HR acts as a filter; instead, message the people directly affected by the lack of employees (e.g., founders or engineers).
- **Evidence Over Experience:** You don't need a deep resume if you can provide immediate evidence of value by building something in the company's domain.
- **Capitalize on Funding Rounds:** Companies that recently raised money (Pre-Seed to Series A) are in high-growth mode but may lack established recruiting pipelines.
- **Leverage AI for Speed:** Use AI tools (Cursor, Lovable, Claude) to quickly build domain-specific tools or projects to show founders.

## Detailed Breakdown / Step-by-Step

- **Slide 1: Intro:** 7 ways to actually land an internship late in the season. Context: Author is a Backend SWE @ HubSpot with 11x offers in 6 months.
- **Slide 2: Find Hidden Job Boards:**
  1. Go to **workatastartup.com**.
  2. Filter by size.
  3. Reach out to 5 you actually care about.
- **Slide 3: Stop Reaching Out to HR:**
  1. Use LinkedIn search for "tech companies".
  2. Filter for small companies (**11-50 employees**).
  3. Message the founders or team leads directly.
- **Slide 4: Build Something in Their Domain:**
  1. Use AI (Cursor, Lovable, Claude) to build a small tool related to the startup.
  2. Use ChatGPT to brainstorm: "domain related tooling for [startup]".
  3. Send the project to the founder as your "application".
- **Slide 5: Target Recently Funded Companies:**
  1. Use **CrunchBase** to find Pre-seed to Series A companies.
  2. Filter for funding in the **last 60 days**.
  3. DM them all; they have money and need to grow fast but may not have a pipeline yet.
- **Slide 6: Conclusion:** Recap of success (11x offers, avg time to offer < 2 weeks). Call to action to comment "INTERN" for a full guide.
