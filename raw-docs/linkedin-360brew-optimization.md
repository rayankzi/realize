# Master LinkedIn's 360Brew Algorithm to 2x Recruiter Inbounds

**Topic/Category:** Career Development / LinkedIn Optimization
**Primary Goal of the Post:** To teach users how to optimize their LinkedIn profiles for the new "360Brew" AI algorithm, moving beyond simple keyword stuffing to semantic relevance and topic clusters.

---

## Executive Summary

LinkedIn has introduced a new foundation model called **360Brew**, which shifts recruiter searches from literal keyword matching to semantic ranking. This means the algorithm now evaluates profiles based on overall relevance, topic clusters, and domain context. To stay visible in a tough job market, users must strategically build a "keyword bank" from live job descriptions and place those terms across specific high-weight sections of their profile (Headline, About, Experience, and Skills).

## Key Insights & Main Ideas

- **The Algorithm Shift:** LinkedIn's 360Brew model uses semantic ranking to match candidates based on fit, topic clusters, and outcomes, not just exact word matches.
- **Topic Clusters > Keywords:** Recruiter searches now look for a combination of topic clusters, outcomes, domain context, and engagement.
- **The Power of Skills:** Profiles with 5+ skills are **27x more likely** to surface in recruiter searches according to LinkedIn's own data.
- **Data-Driven Optimization:** Use search appearance analytics to verify if your keyword strategy is actually working.

## Detailed Breakdown / Step-by-Step

### 1. Build Your Keyword Bank
- **Collect:** Gather 8–12 live Job Descriptions (JDs) for roles you could realistically land in 3–9 months.
- **Identify:** Highlight every term that repeats across 3+ JDs.
- **Categorize:** Look for terms in 4 categories:
    - **Hard Skills:** Tools, platforms, methods.
    - **Domain:** Industry, vertical, business model.
    - **Scope:** Team size, budget, geography.
    - **Outcomes:** Strong verbs like launched, scaled, owned, P&L'd.

### 2. Place Them Strategically
- **Headline:** Use all 220 characters. Front-load the first 70 characters (visible in mobile search). This is the highest-weighted field for matching.
- **About:** The first 300 characters are critical (visible before the "see more" button).
- **Experience:** Include 3–5 keywords per role *inside* the bullet points, not just in the job title.
- **Skills:** Add 15–20 specific skills and pin your top 3.

### 3. Verify, Analyse, and Iterate
- Navigate to: **Profile -> Show all analytics -> Search appearances**.
- This free tool shows how many recruiter searches you appeared in last week and the job titles of the people who found you.
- Monitor these numbers to see if your new keywords have "landed."
