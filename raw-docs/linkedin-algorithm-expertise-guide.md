# Mastering the New LinkedIn Expertise-Weighted Algorithm

**Topic/Category:** Social Media Strategy / LinkedIn Growth
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To explain LinkedIn's shift to an expertise-weighted algorithm and identify the content formats that drive the highest engagement under this new system.

---

## Executive Summary

LinkedIn has moved away from a simple engagement-based system to a sophisticated 150-billion parameter AI model that prioritizes expertise and credibility. This new "expertise-weighted" algorithm functions like a human editor, evaluating whether a creator is truly qualified to speak on a topic. Reach is now heavily influenced by deep engagement signals like saves and long comments rather than just likes or hashtags.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **AI-Driven Expertise:** The new algorithm uses a massive AI model to understand post topics and verify the creator's credibility.
- **Deep Engagement Over Reactions:** Likes are less valuable. Saves now drive 5x more reach, and comments of three or more sentences are weighted 15x heavier than simple reactions.
- **Engagement Quality Matters:** The algorithm prioritizes "dwell time" and meaningful interactions, signaling that the content is worth pushing to a broader audience.
- **Content Type Performance:** Traditional text posts are losing ground to formats that require more time from the user, specifically video and carousels.
