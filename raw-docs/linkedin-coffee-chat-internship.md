# How to Land an Internship in 2 Weeks Using LinkedIn Coffee Chats

**Topic/Category:** Career Advice / Internship Search / Networking
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** Teach students a step-by-step strategy for leveraging their university alumni network on LinkedIn to secure an internship through coffee chats and referrals.

---

## Executive Summary

This reel provides a no-nonsense, actionable playbook for students who still haven't secured a summer internship. The creator — who landed an internship in Paris as an international student in roughly two weeks — walks through a LinkedIn alumni networking strategy. The core idea: connect with alumni at target companies, convert connections into informal coffee chats, treat those chats as personality-first conversations (not interviews), demonstrate value, and secure a referral that gets your application past automated screening.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Insight 1:** Your university alumni network on LinkedIn is an underutilized shortcut — filter by school, then by target company or role to find warm leads.
- **Insight 2:** Coffee chats are informal interviews in disguise. The person evaluates your personality first and technical skills second. If they can't hire you directly, they'll refer you to someone who can.
- **Insight 3:** Volume matters — aim for 50 to 100 connection requests to account for low response rates and ensure you get enough coffee chats.
- **Insight 4:** Always demonstrate value during the chat. Bring something tangible — a marketing campaign concept, a small app, a bug fix — to prove you can contribute from day one.
- **Insight 5:** A referral from an insider guarantees your application is reviewed by a real person instead of being filtered out by an ATS bot.

## Narrative & Step-by-Step Breakdown

1. **Go to LinkedIn** and search for your university.
2. **Filter alumni** by the company name or position title you're targeting (e.g., "software engineer," "finance analyst").
3. **Send a connection request** with a personalized note (template offered via comment engagement — see Verbatim Templates below).
4. **Once they accept**, message them asking for a 15-minute coffee chat. Frame it around their career journey being inspiring and wanting to learn how they landed their role.
5. **Prepare for the coffee chat** by researching ("stalking") their LinkedIn profile — learn their interests, current projects, and background.
6. **During the chat**, treat it like meeting a friend for coffee, not a formal interview. Discuss their interests and what they're working on.
7. **At the end, show your value.** Bring a deliverable relevant to the role:
   - Marketing → share campaign ideas you've created
   - Computer Science → build a small app or identify/fix a bug on their company's site
8. **Make your ask crystal clear.** State that you're looking for an internship, specify the duration and intended position.
9. **If they like you**, they'll refer you to a recruiter or HR, ensuring your application is reviewed by a human.
