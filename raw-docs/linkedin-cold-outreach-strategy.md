# LinkedIn Cold Outreach Strategy for Hidden Jobs

**Topic/Category:** Career Advice / Job Search Strategy
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To teach viewers how to use LinkedIn filters to find small companies that are more likely to respond to direct outreach and how to find decision-makers' contact information.

---

## Executive Summary

This video provides a tactical, step-by-step guide for job seekers to bypass traditional job boards and "Apply" buttons. The creator argues that emailing 100 people at specific small-to-medium-sized startups (1-50 employees) is a high-probability way to secure an interview. The strategy involves using LinkedIn's advanced filters to identify these companies by industry and location, then using an email finder tool to contact founders and hiring managers directly.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Target Small Teams:** Companies with 1-50 employees are the most responsive because they often lack the rigid, standardized hiring processes of larger corporations.
- **Bypass Job Boards:** Direct emailing decision-makers is presented as a faster and more effective route to an interview than applying through job boards.
- **The "100 Emails" Rule:** The creator posits that sending 100 personalized cold emails virtually guarantees at least one life-changing interview.
- **Decision Maker Direct:** Focus outreach on founders, recruiters, and managers found in the "People" tab of a company's LinkedIn page.
- **Use Email Finders:** Tools can be used to convert LinkedIn profiles into direct contact information (emails and sometimes phone numbers).

## Narrative & Step-by-Step Breakdown

1.  **LinkedIn Search:** Open LinkedIn and start with a blank search, then filter by **Companies**.
2.  **Apply Filters:**
    *   **Industry:** Select your target field (e.g., Financial Services).
    *   **Company Size:** Select "1 to 10" and "11 to 50".
    *   **Location:** Narrow down to your desired city (e.g., London).
3.  **Identify Decision Makers:** Click on a company from the filtered list and navigate to the **People** tab. Look for founders, recruiters, and junior managers.
4.  **Find Contact Info:** Use an email finder tool (the creator mentions one is available in their guide) to get their professional email addresses.
5.  **Outreach:** Send your email and resume to these individuals.
