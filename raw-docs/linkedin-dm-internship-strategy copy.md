# The LinkedIn DM Strategy to Land Internships Without a CV

**Topic/Category:** Career Development / Job Search Strategy
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To teach students and job seekers how to bypass traditional application systems (AI screening) by using a specific high-converting LinkedIn cold DM framework.

---

## Executive Summary

Faraz Ahmad shares his successful LinkedIn outreach strategy that helped him land two internships and receive multiple offers without ever submitting a CV. He argues that traditional application portals, where CVs are screened by AI, are a "race to the bottom." Instead, he advocates for a value-driven, direct message (DM) approach that respects the recipient's time and focuses on being "useful" rather than asking for a job.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Bypass the AI:** Traditional CV screening is a bottleneck. Direct DMs allow you to skip the line and talk directly to decision-makers.
- **Respect the Scan:** People don't read messages; they scan them. Your structure must be scannable and concise.
- **Value Over Request:** Don't ask for a job or mentorship. Offer value and state your availability to be "useful."
- **Proof Points:** Use one strong accomplishment to trigger curiosity and profile clicks.
- **Consistency Wins:** Spending just 20 minutes a day on this strategy can yield responses by the end of the week.

## Narrative & Step-by-Step Breakdown

- **Beginning:** The Hook. Faraz reveals he landed two internships and turned down nine offers without a CV. He identifies the enemy: AI screening bots at the bottom of a 1,000-CV pile.
- **Middle:** The 5-Step DM Structure:
    1. **Personalization:** Start with their name and the phrase "Super quickly" to signal respect for their time.
    2. **Acknowledge the Cold Message:** Be transparent that you haven't met.
    3. **The Proof Point:** Briefly state who you are and one impressive thing you've done.
    4. **The Offer:** State your availability and your desire to be useful (not seeking a job).
    5. **The Soft Ask:** Request a 15-minute intro call.
- **End:** The Guarantee & CTA. Faraz guarantees results with 20 minutes of daily effort and asks viewers to comment "message" for the template.
