# Unpatched Internship Strategy: 11x Offers via LinkedIn DMs

**Topic/Category:** Career Advice / Job Search Strategy
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To teach students and job seekers how to bypass AI resume screenings and land internships by using a specific LinkedIn DM outreach structure.

---

## Executive Summary

The creator argues that the 2026 job market is "completely cooked" for those relying on mass-applying with CVs. He shares his "unpatched" strategy of skipping the standard application process entirely by DMing people on LinkedIn, which landed him two internships and nine other offers without sending a single CV. The core of the strategy is a highly optimized message structure that respects the recipient's time and focuses on offering value rather than asking for a job.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Stop Mass Applying:** AI-driven CV screening is a bottleneck; skipping it via direct outreach is more effective.
- **Respect the Scan:** People don't read DMs; they scan them. Your message must be optimized for quick consumption.
- **Acknowledge the Cold Message:** Being transparent about not having met before builds more trust than pretending otherwise.
- **Lead with Value:** Instead of seeking mentorship or a job, offer your availability and skills to be useful to the recipient.
- **The "Super Quickly" Phrase:** Using this phrase signals that you respect their time, increasing the likelihood of a read.

## Narrative & Step-by-Step Breakdown

1. **The Hook (Beginning):** Bold claim that the job market is "cooked" and mass applying is a mistake, backed by personal proof of landing 11 offers without a CV.
2. **The Problem:** Being at the bottom of a list of 1,000 CVs waiting for AI screening.
3. **The Solution:** DMing people on LinkedIn using a specific 5-point structure.
4. **The Step-by-Step DM Structure (Middle):**
    - **Step 1:** Start with their name and "super quickly."
    - **Step 2:** Acknowledge it's a cold message ("We've never met").
    - **Step 3:** Brief intro with one strong proof point (curiosity gap).
    - **Step 4:** State availability and offer to be useful (value proposition).
    - **Step 5:** Soft ask for a 15-minute intro call.
5. **The Conclusion (End):** Guarantee of results if practiced daily (20 mins/day) and a Call to Action to get the exact template.
