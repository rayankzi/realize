## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Insight 1:** Your LinkedIn banner is prime real estate — use it to communicate your target job title, key skills, and a unique value proposition (e.g., "Turning data insights into business value").
- **Insight 2:** Headlines must be recruiter-searchable. Put a standardized job title first, followed by relevant skills/certifications and your value proposition. Avoid gimmicky titles like "Chief Rock Star."
- **Insight 3:** Having 500+ connections signals to recruiters that you're active on the platform and likely to respond to outreach, making them more inclined to send opportunities.
- **Insight 4:** Experience descriptions should follow the **XYZ format**: "I accomplished **X** as measured by **Y** by doing **Z**." Descriptions that only list responsibilities or describe the company are insufficient.
- **Insight 5:** Your skills section should feature specific technical/hard skills relevant to your target job descriptions — not generic soft skills like "communication." Order them by relevance to maximize endorsement visibility.
