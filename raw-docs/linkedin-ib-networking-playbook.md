## Executive Summary

This reel lays out a tactical, repeatable LinkedIn networking playbook for breaking into investment banking. The creator walks through a full outreach funnel — from identifying alumni at target banks, to sending connection requests with a specific template, to following up after acceptance to land coffee chats, to asking the right questions during those calls, and finally leveraging those conversations to get introduced to other bankers at the firm. The core thesis: this systematic approach exponentially increases your odds of getting referrals and interviews during IB recruiting season.

- **Insight 4:** Timing matters — the creator emphasizes "it's crunch time," signaling this is most effective when executed during active recruiting windows.
