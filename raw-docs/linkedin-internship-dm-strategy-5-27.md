# How to Land Internships via LinkedIn DMs (Without a CV)

**Topic/Category:** Career Advice / Job Search Strategy
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To teach students and job seekers how to bypass AI-screened CV filters by using a specific, high-conversion LinkedIn DM structure to land internships.

---

## Executive Summary

The creator reveals a strategy that helped him land two internships and receive nine additional offers by skipping traditional applications and directly messaging recruiters and founders on LinkedIn. The core takeaway is a five-step DM framework designed to respect the recipient's time, establish immediate credibility, and offer value rather than asking for a job, which he claims "7x'ed" his reply rate.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Bypass the AI:** Traditional CV applications often get stuck in AI screening filters; DMing allows you to skip this line entirely.
- **Respect Time:** Using the phrase "super quickly" signals that you won't waste the recipient's time, making them more likely to scan the message.
- **The "Value First" Mindset:** Instead of asking for mentorship or a job, position yourself as someone with a few months of availability who wants to "be useful" to their team.
- **Consistency is Key:** Spending just 20 minutes a day sending these DMs can lead to responses by the end of the week.
- **Credibility over Biography:** You only need one strong "proof point" to make them curious enough to check your profile.

## Narrative & Step-by-Step Breakdown

1. **The Hook:** The creator starts by sharing his success—landing two internships and turning down nine offers—without ever sending a CV.
2. **The Problem:** He explains the frustration of being at the bottom of a list of 1,000 CVs waiting for AI screening.
3. **The Solution:** A step-by-step breakdown of his "Every Structure" for LinkedIn DMs:
    - **Step 1: Start with their name and "Super quickly"** (Signals respect for time).
    - **Step 2: Acknowledge the cold message** (Uses "We've never met").
    - **Step 3: State who you are and one proof point** (Builds curiosity).
    - **Step 4: State intended outcome** (Mention availability and desire to be useful).
    - **Step 5: Use a "SOFT" ask only** (A 15-minute intro call).
4. **Call to Action:** Encourages viewers to comment "message" for his exact template and follow for daily updates.
