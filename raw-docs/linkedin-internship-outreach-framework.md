# How to Land Internships via LinkedIn DM (No CV Needed)

**Topic/Category:** Career Development / Job Search Strategy
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To teach viewers a specific LinkedIn DM framework for landing internships by bypassing traditional AI-screened CV applications and offering direct value instead.

---

## Executive Summary

The creator explains how he landed two internships and turned down nine other offers by skipping the standard CV screening process and directly messaging recruiters or startup founders on LinkedIn. He provides a five-step "super quick" outreach framework that emphasizes respecting the recipient's time, acknowledging the cold nature of the message, providing a single "proof point" for credibility, offering immediate value rather than asking for a job, and concluding with a low-pressure "soft ask" for a short call.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Bypass AI Screeners:** Traditional CV applications often get stuck in AI-driven screening queues; direct LinkedIn DMs allow you to skip this entire stage.
- **Value-First Approach:** Instead of asking for a job or mentorship, frame your outreach as an offer to be useful for the next few months.
- **Respect Time:** Using the phrase "super quickly" signals to busy professionals that you aren't going to waste their time.
- **Proof Points:** You only need one strong proof point to make someone curious enough to check your LinkedIn profile.
- **Consistency is Key:** Spending just 20 minutes a day on this outreach strategy can yield responses within a week.

## Narrative & Step-by-Step Breakdown

### The 5-Step Outreach Framework

1.  **Start with Name + "Super Quickly":** Signals respect for their time and caters to the way people scan messages rather than reading them in depth.
2.  **Acknowledge it's a Cold Message:** Be transparent that you haven't met before (e.g., "We've never met").
3.  **Identify Yourself + One Proof Point:** Briefly state who you are and one achievement or skill that builds credibility (e.g., "CS Undergrad @ KCL", "Built/scaled communities").
4.  **State Intended Outcome (Offer Value):** Mention your availability (e.g., "Have the next 3 months free") and explicitly state you want to be "useful" to them. Avoid asking for a job or mentorship immediately.
5.  **The Soft Ask:** Finish with a low-stakes request, such as a "15-minute intro call."
