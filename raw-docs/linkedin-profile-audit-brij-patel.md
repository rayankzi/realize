# Rate My LinkedIn: Brij Patel's Profile Audit

**Topic/Category:** Career Development / LinkedIn Optimization
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To provide a rapid-fire audit of a student's LinkedIn profile, highlighting common mistakes like boring headlines and lack of activity while offering actionable improvements.

---

## Executive Summary

This reel features a constructive but blunt audit of Brij Patel's LinkedIn profile. While the profile starts strong with a professional headshot and "gorgeous" banner, it falters significantly in its headline, activity level, and "About" section. The reviewer emphasizes that simply listing a major and school is a wasted opportunity, especially when the user has high-value internship experience at State Farm that should be highlighted upfront. The audit concludes with a mediocre 5/10 rating, primarily due to the "boring" headline and lack of engagement on the platform.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Headlines Matter:** Your headline should not just be your major and school. It’s the most visible part of your profile; use it to highlight current roles (like an internship at State Farm) or specific value propositions.
- **Activity is "Aura":** Having zero posts and no "About Me" section is described as a "serious loss of aura." A profile without activity looks stagnant to recruiters.
- **Max Out Your Skills:** Having only 9 skills is insufficient for a professional profile. The reviewer encourages "locking in" and adding more relevant skills to improve searchability.
- **Eliminate Redundancy:** If your organizations or leadership roles are already listed in your Experience or Education sections, they don't necessarily need a dedicated "Organizations" section if they don't add new information.
- **Highlight Unique Experience:** Being a TA for a class like "Love and Persuasion" is an "intrigued" point of interest—unique roles should be elaborated on to spark conversation.

## Narrative & Step-by-Step Breakdown

- **Beginning (The Hook):** The video starts with immediate praise for the visual elements—the banner and the headshot—before quickly pivoting to the "terrible" and "boring" headline.
- **Middle (The Deep Dive):** The reviewer scrolls through the profile, noting the missing "About Me" section and lack of posts. They then examine the Experience section, acknowledging the State Farm internship and the unique TA role.
- **End (The Verdict):** A quick check of the Education and Skills sections leads to the final recommendation: fixing the headline should be the top priority. The video ends with a "5 out of 10" score overlay.
