# How to Make Recruiters Beg to Hire You: The Jess Ramos LinkedIn Strategy

**Topic/Category:** Career Development / LinkedIn Personal Branding
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To teach job seekers how to leverage LinkedIn's personal branding power to shift from "applying for jobs" to "being recruited" by performing three daily habits.

---

## Executive Summary

Jess Ramos, a prominent tech influencer with over 450k followers, explains why she hasn't applied for a job in years. By treating LinkedIn as a personal marketing platform rather than a static resume, she attracts recruiters and CEOs directly to her DMs. The core takeaway is that consistent niche-specific content, active engagement, and a polished profile create a "career landing page" that builds authority and makes the job search process inbound rather than outbound.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Personal Branding > Job Applications:** High-volume job applications are seen as "desperate," whereas a strong personal brand creates demand.
- **LinkedIn as a Marketing Tool:** Success on LinkedIn comes from "tricking" (marketing) others into recognizing your expertise through visibility and authority.
- **Consistency is Key:** The strategy requires daily action—posting, engaging, and updating—to maintain high visibility in the job market.
- **Inbound Opportunities:** A well-optimized profile and consistent presence lead to high-quality inbound job offers from recruiters and executives.

## Narrative & Step-by-Step Breakdown

The video follows a fast-paced "talking head" format with on-screen text overlays and screen recordings of LinkedIn:

1.  **The Hook:** Jess opens with the bold claim that she hasn't applied for a job in years because she makes recruiters "beg to hire her."
2.  **The Evidence:** She shows a screen recording of her LinkedIn profile (Jess Ramos - 450k+ followers) and a sample DM from a "Verified Recruiter" offering a Developer Advocate role.
3.  **The 3-Step System:**
    -   **Step 1: Post Thought Leadership content.** Post relevant niche content to build authority. She shows examples of her own posts about data science and tech trends.
    -   **Step 2: Engage with others.** Build real relationships through comments and DMs. She emphasizes that "great minds think alike."
    -   **Step 3: Update your profile.** Treat your profile as a "career landing page" that showcases current achievements and projects.
4.  **Conclusion:** A final warning: "If you're not working LinkedIn, LinkedIn might be working you."
