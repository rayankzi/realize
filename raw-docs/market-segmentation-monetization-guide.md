## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Teenagers (13-19) buy status:** They are focused on aesthetic, belonging, and identity. To sell to them, focus on positioning and narrative.
- **Young Adults (20-30) buy skills and shortcuts:** This is the easiest group to monetize due to high desire and low patience. They want to escape "average" through income-increasing skills (coding, AI, editing) and automation.
- **Mid-Career (30-45) buy time:** They have the capital but lack the hours. Convenience and stability are their primary drivers.
- **Older Market (45+) buy security:** They value authority and proof over flashy branding. They want to protect their legacy and maintain their health.
- **Business ROI is the ultimate hook:** Service-based businesses, E-commerce, Info products, and SaaS all have unique needs, but the fastest path to high income is selling clear ROI (leads, growth, or scalability).
