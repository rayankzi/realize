## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Markov Chains in trading:** The probability of the market's next state (bullish or bearish) is determined solely by its current state — this is the foundation of the strategy.
- **Entry/exit logic:** Enter when the market flips from bearish to bullish; exit as soon as the bullish trend is lost. Simple, rules-based, no discretion.
- **State classification:** The market state (bullish vs. bearish) is derived from the price change relative to the previous ticker price.
- **Automated backtesting:** The creator auto-backtested the strategy before risking real capital, a critical step for any algo.
- **Live deployment:** The strategy was deployed directly to E-Trade, bridging theory to real-money execution.
- **Results:** 56%+ return, Sharpe ratio of 0.74 — a solid risk-adjusted performance metric for a simple strategy.
