# Master Class in Handling Price Objections & Changing Call Dynamics

**Topic/Category:** Sales Training / High-Ticket Sales
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To demonstrate how to professionally push back on a prospect who tries to skip straight to the price, effectively resetting the power dynamic and qualifying the lead.

---

## Executive Summary

This reel captures a high-intensity moment in a sales call where a prospect tries to bypass the discovery phase by demanding the price within the first 60 seconds. The salesperson expertly uses "negative reverse selling" and radical honesty to regain control. By calling out the behavior directly and even offering to end the call, he shifts the dynamic from a commodity-based transaction to a value-based partnership, eventually getting the prospect to apologize and open up about the actual business bottlenecks.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Don't Be Afraid to Walk Away:** The salesperson explicitly states, "Let's just end the call here," when the prospect devalues the discovery process. This demonstrates high status and abundance.
- **Humanize the Interaction:** By saying, "You seem like a good dude... I can tell you have good intention," the salesperson lowers the prospect's guard while maintaining a firm stance.
- **The "Me" Factor:** Challenging the prospect's assumptions by asking, "The people you might have spoken to in the past... were they me?" effectively separates the salesperson from past bad experiences the prospect may have had.
- **Address "Lead Quality" vs. "Sales Process":** The conversation pivots from a price discussion to a deep dive into why the sales team is "blowing" leads, identifying the real problem as conversion rather than lead volume.

## Narrative & Step-by-Step Breakdown

- **Beginning (The Conflict):** The prospect demands the price 60 seconds in, claiming discovery is a "waste of time."
- **Middle (The Pivot):** The salesperson calls out the disrespect to the process. He explains that he cannot help more than the prospect wants to be helped and builds a plan for a "productive conversation."
- **Middle (The Reversal):** The salesperson asks how the prospect can know the questions are unnecessary before they've even been asked. The prospect concedes, "You're right," and apologizes.
- **End (Discovery):** The dynamic is reset. The salesperson moves into discovery, and the prospect reveals that they have too many leads but low closing rates (the "reps are blowing them").

## Verbatim Templates & Scripts

### Resetting the Call Dynamic Script
> "We're going to need to change the dynamic of this conversation, mate. Because if you think training your team to increase their conversion rate, increase the profitability of the business is a waste of time, then I'm the first person to tell you, let's just end the call here."

### Handling "Unnecessary Questions" Objection
> "I understand that. But the people you might have spoken to in the past that made you feel like they were unnecessary, were they me? No. Like, how do you even know what I'm going to ask you when we just hopped on a call 60 seconds ago and you have no clue who I am?"

### The "I'm Not a Scammer" Reassurance
> "I'll make it quick, but I still need to understand enough about the business to be able to know, like, great, can we even help you in the first place? Because I'm not here to scam you. That's not my intention. Cool?"
