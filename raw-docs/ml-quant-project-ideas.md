## Narrative & Step-by-Step Breakdown

The reel presents a numbered list of 10 project ideas, each briefly described:

1. **Alpha Factor Discovery** – Build ML models to uncover predictive trading signals beyond basic indicators like RSI.
2. **Limit Order Book Prediction** – Use LSTMs or Transformers on order flow data to forecast short-term price moves.
3. **Reinforcement Learning Trader** – Create an agent that dynamically adjusts a portfolio to maximize returns while managing risk.
4. **Options Pricing Model** – Train neural networks to estimate option prices and compare against Black-Scholes.
5. **News Sentiment Trading System** – Use NLP models on financial news or earnings calls to generate trading signals.
6. **Volatility Forecasting** – Predict future market volatility with time-series ML models for risk management.
7. **Pairs Trading Strategy** – Detect co-integrated assets and use ML to automate a mean-reversion trading strategy.
8. **Fraud Detection for Transactions** – Build anomaly detection models to spot suspicious trading or payment activity.
9. **Portfolio Optimization with ML** – Apply supervised or reinforcement learning to dynamically allocate asset weights.
10. **Alternative Data Alpha** – Analyze unconventional data sources like satellite images, credit card spend, or social media to generate trading signals.
