# How to Stand Out for Top AI Internships (NVIDIA Case Study)

**Topic/Category:** Career Advice / CS Internships / AI
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To explain the specific criteria "top companies" (like NVIDIA) use to select interns in 2026, emphasizing practical AI building, agency, and communication.

---

## Executive Summary

This reel features an NVIDIA employee discussing the selection process for their summer interns. With over 4,000 applicants for just two spots, the bar is exceptionally high. The speaker highlights that while GPA and school prestige still matter, the defining factors are proactive building with AI tools, individual agency, and strong communication skills. He also strongly advocates for in-person work early in one's career to maximize growth.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Build with AI:** It's not about which languages you know; it's about what you've actually built using AI and how you integrate it into your workflow.
- **Agency is Key:** Candidates must demonstrate the ability to identify a problem, build a solution, and market that solution independently.
- **Master Modern Tools:** Successful interns are productive with AI-native development tools like Codex, Cursor, and Claude Code.
- **Communication & Charisma:** Being able to communicate ideas clearly and hold a charismatic conversation is a major differentiator. Technical skills are the baseline; communication is the edge.
- **GPA & School Still Matter:** Despite common claims, high GPAs from top-tier schools remain important filtering criteria for elite companies.
- **In-Office Presence:** Working in person, especially early in a career, is seen as vital. The speaker warns that remote work early on is "shooting yourself in the foot."

## Narrative & Step-by-Step Breakdown

- **Beginning (Hook):** Announces that NVIDIA summer interns are joining next week and uses the extreme competition (4,000+ apps for 2 spots) to establish the value of the advice.
- **Middle (Core Criteria):**
    1. **Practical AI Usage:** Focus on building and workflows over language knowledge.
    2. **Agency:** The trifecta of Problem identification -> Building -> Marketing.
    3. **Tool Proficiency:** Specific mention of being productive with AI tools (Codex, Cursor, Claude Code).
    4. **Soft Skills:** Communication, clarity, and charisma are emphasized as being just as important as technical builds.
- **End (The Truths & Advice):** Acknowledges the "uncomfortable truth" that GPA and school prestige matter. Closes with a strong endorsement of in-office work for career growth.
