# Passive Job Seeker Strategy: How to Make Recruiters Come to You

**Topic/Category:** Career Development / Job Search Strategy
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To introduce the "Passive Job Seeker Series" and explain the shift from active, desperate job searching to a passive approach where companies reach out to the candidate.

---

## Executive Summary

The creator shares their experience of not submitting a job application in 18 months yet receiving calls from top companies like Google, Amazon, and Plaid. They contrast the traditional "fire drill" approach to job searching—characterized by mass applications and zero leverage—with a new "passive" strategy focused on reachability and inbound interest. This video serves as an introduction to a series documenting what works and what doesn't in making yourself a magnet for recruiters.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **The "Fire Drill" Trap:** Treating job searching as an emergency (e.g., due to layoffs or visa deadlines) leads to mass applications, cold outreach, and zero leverage.
- **Leverage is Key:** When you are desperate, your leverage is zero. Passive searching builds leverage by attracting opportunities before they are needed.
- **Reachability over Applications:** Instead of applying to companies, focus on making yourself "reachable" so that companies come to you.
- **Continuous Documentation:** The creator is documenting their transition and experiments in real-time to show both successful and unsuccessful tactics.

## Narrative & Step-by-Step Breakdown

- **Beginning (The Hook):** The creator reveals they haven't applied for a job in 18 months but have a backlog of recruiter calls from major tech firms.
- **Middle (The Problem):** Analysis of the old way of job searching—waiting for a crisis (layoff/visa deadline) and then scrambling with referrals and LinkedIn updates.
- **End (The Pivot):** Introducing the "Passive Job Seeker Series" where the focus shifts from active applications to inbound reachability.
