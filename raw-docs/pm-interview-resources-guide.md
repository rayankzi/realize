# Best Resources for Landing a Product Management (PM) Job

**Topic/Category:** Career / Product Management
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To provide a curated list of high-value resources for aspiring Product Managers to prepare for interviews and land roles.

---

## Executive Summary

This reel highlights four essential resources that the creator used to successfully land a Product Management (PM) position. The content focuses on a mix of video learning, structured drills, mock interview practice, and a comprehensive guidebook, emphasizing a practical and thorough approach to PM interview preparation.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Visual Learning is Key:** Binge-watching specialized PM YouTube channels like Exponent can provide deep context on various PM roles and interview styles (AI for PMs, SWE to PM, etc.).
- **Structured Practice:** Using platforms like Rocketblocks for "product drills" helps in honing specific skills required for PM interviews.
- **Peer & Professional Feedback:** Engaging in mock interviews with both peers and experienced PMs (via Exponent Mocks) is crucial for building confidence and receiving actionable feedback.
- **Comprehensive Theory:** Following a structured guide, such as Will Lawrence's APM Guidebook, ensures all aspects of the PM recruitment process are covered, from understanding programs to crushing the interview.
