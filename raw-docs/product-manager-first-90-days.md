# How to Thrive in Your First 90 Days as a Product Manager

**Topic/Category:** Career Development / Product Management
**Primary Goal of the Post:** To provide actionable advice for new Product Managers on how to effectively navigate and succeed during their first three months in a new role.

---

## Executive Summary

This post, by Google/Amazon Product Manager Michael Yitayew (@productmanagermike), outlines the most critical priorities for a new PM's first 90 days. Instead of rushing to launch features or change directions, the post emphasizes building a strong foundation through relationship building, supporting existing execution, and developing deep product empathy by acting as a user.

## Key Insights & Main Ideas

- **Relationship Building is Paramount:** Focus on the Engineering and UX teams early on. Listen more than you speak.
- **Earn the Right to Lead Vision:** Do not attempt to redirect the product vision immediately; this responsibility is earned through trust and execution.
- **Value is Found in Unblocking Others:** Support the team by handling roadmap questions, helping with design trade-offs, and removing obstacles for others.
- **Deep Product Empathy:** True understanding comes from a combination of using the product yourself, reading documentation, and observing real user interactions.

- **Slide 5: Priority #3 - Be the user.**
    - Deepen understanding by observing user interactions.
    - Read all available documentation.
    - Use the product extensively yourself.
