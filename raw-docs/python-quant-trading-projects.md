## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Quant trading is probabilistic, not predictive:** The Monte Carlo simulator teaches the mental model shift from "what will the price be?" to "what is the distribution of possible futures?"
- **Assumptions kill strategies:** A backtesting engine exposes how transaction costs, slippage, and volatility regime changes break ideas that look good on paper.
- **Risk-adjusted exposure matters more than raw returns:** The volatility targeting model shows how real funds size positions dynamically to maintain consistent risk levels.
- **Diversification is about correlation, not count:** Factor model analysis reveals that holding more assets doesn't reduce risk unless their correlation structure is understood.
- **Market microstructure shapes price:** Even a simplified order book model demonstrates how spreads, inventory risk, and liquidity affect actual trade execution.
