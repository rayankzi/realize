# How to Message a Recruiter and Actually Get a Response

**Topic/Category:** Career Coaching / Job Search Strategy / Networking
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** Teach job seekers how to craft effective recruiter outreach messages that get responses, by replacing generic follow-ups with intentional, personalized templates.

---

## Executive Summary

Most people's recruiter messages fail because they put all the burden on the recruiter and give them nothing to respond to. This reel contrasts two common (ineffective) message types with two proven alternatives — one for applicants following up on a role, one for cold outreach without a specific application. The creator positions these templates as the difference between being ignored and starting a real conversation that leads to interviews.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Generic follow-ups get ignored:** Messages like "Just following up on my application" or "Do you have any roles for me?" offer no value and create work for the recruiter.
- **Specificity signals effort:** Mentioning the exact role title, 1–2 relevant skills, and something specific about the team's work makes you stand out immediately.
- **Frame it as a conversation, not a transaction:** Asking to "connect and learn more" removes pressure and makes it easy for the recruiter to respond.
- **Cold outreach works too:** Even without applying, expressing genuine interest in the company's work and your relevant background can open doors.
- **Most candidates only apply — the ones getting interviews are starting conversations.**

## Narrative & Step-by-Step Breakdown

- **Beginning (Hook):** Opens with a relatable pain point — messaging a recruiter and hearing nothing back — then reframes it: it's not you, it's *how* you're reaching out.
- **Middle (Problem):** Shows two examples of bad messages that get ignored, explaining why each fails (puts pressure on recruiter, gives nothing to respond to).
- **End (Solution):** Delivers two ready-to-use message templates (applied vs. not applied), explains why they work, and closes with a soft pitch for 1:1 job search coaching.

## Verbatim Templates & Scripts

**Option 1 — If You've Already Applied:**

> Hi [Name],
> I came across the [Role Title] position and wanted to reach out directly.
>
> I have experience in [1–2 relevant skills], and I'm especially interested in the work your team is doing around [specific detail].
>
> I'd love to connect and learn more about what you're looking for in this role - and share a bit more about my background.
>
> Thanks so much,
> [Your Name]

---

**Option 2 — If You Haven't Applied (Cold Outreach):**

> Hi [Name],
> I've been following [Company/Team] and really admire the work you're doing in [specific area].
>
> I have a background in [relevant experience], and I'm currently exploring opportunities in this space.
>
> I'd love to connect and learn more about your team and what you look for when hiring.
>
> Thanks so much,
> [Your Name]
