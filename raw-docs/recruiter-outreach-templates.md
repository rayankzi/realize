# Effective Recruiter Outreach Templates

**Topic/Category:** Career Development / Job Search Strategy
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To teach job seekers how to message recruiters effectively by replacing low-effort "follow-up" messages with intentional, value-driven templates that increase response rates.

---

## Executive Summary

Many job seekers struggle to get responses from recruiters because their initial outreach puts too much pressure on the recruiter without providing enough context or value. This video identifies common mistakes—such as asking for any open roles or sending vague follow-ups—and provides two specific "Option" templates designed to start genuine conversations. By showing effort and specific interest in the team's work, candidates can move from being ignored to getting interviews.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Avoid the "Burden" Message:** Never ask a recruiter if they have "any roles that would be a good fit." This forces them to do the work of matching you to a role, which often leads to being ignored.
- **Intentionality Wins:** Show that you've researched the company and have a specific reason for reaching out beyond just wanting a job.
- **Connect, Don't Just Apply:** The goal of outreach is to learn and share background, not just to "follow up" on an application status.
- **Leverage Specificity:** Mentioning 1-2 relevant skills and a specific detail about the team's work significantly increases the likelihood of a response.

## Narrative & Step-by-Step Breakdown

- **Beginning (The Hook):** The creator identifies the pain point: messaging recruiters and hearing nothing back. She shows common examples of messages that fail and explains *why* they fail (they put pressure on the recruiter).

    1. **Option 1:** Used after applying to a specific role to add a personal touch.
    2. **Option 2:** Used before applying to build a connection based on admiration for the team's work.
- **End (The Why & CTA):** She emphasizes that job seekers who start conversations are the ones getting interviews. She concludes with a call to action for her 1:1 coaching and a prompt to save the reel.

## Verbatim Templates & Scripts

> ### ✅ OPTION 1 (if you applied)
> 
> Hi [Name],
> I came across the [Role Title] position and wanted to reach out directly.
> 
> I have experience in [1–2 relevant skills], and I’m especially interested in the work your team is doing around [specific detail].
> 
> I’d love to connect and learn more about what you’re looking for in this role - and share a bit more about my background.
> 
> Thanks so much,
> [Your Name]

> ### ✅ OPTION 2 (if you haven’t applied)
> 
> Hi [Name],
> I’ve been following [Company/Team] and really admire the work you’re doing in [specific area].
> 
> I have a background in [relevant experience], and I’m currently exploring opportunities in this space.
> 
> I’d love to connect and learn more about your team and what you look for when hiring.
> 
> Thanks so much,
> [Your Name]
