# Top 5 Remote Job Boards That Actually Work

**Topic/Category:** Career / Remote Work / Job Search
**Primary Goal of the Post:** To recommend five legitimate, remote-specific job boards that filter out fake or hybrid-labeled "remote" listings, helping job seekers navigate a shrinking and misleading remote job market.

---

## Executive Summary

With only 8% of job listings being fully remote yet attracting over 40% of all applications, the remote job market is brutally competitive and riddled with misleading postings. This carousel cuts through the noise by recommending five remote-focused job boards that do a better job of curating genuinely remote positions, each with distinct strengths depending on experience level, industry, and geography. The creator positions these as superior alternatives to mainstream platforms like Indeed.

## Key Insights & Main Ideas

- **The remote job market is oversaturated and deceptive:** Only 8% of listings are fully remote, but they receive 40%+ of all applications. Some companies falsely advertise hybrid roles as "remote."
- **Remote-specific job boards filter better:** Niche platforms dedicated to remote work are far more reliable than general job boards for finding legitimate remote positions.
- **Experience level matters when choosing a board:** Some boards cater to senior professionals (5+ or 8+ years), while others are more general.
- **All recommended boards are free to browse:** The platforms charge for AI auto-application features, but the creator advises against using those anyway.
- **Tech companies tend to offer more remote roles:** Boards focused on tech (like Built In and Himalayas) naturally surface a higher percentage of remote opportunities.
