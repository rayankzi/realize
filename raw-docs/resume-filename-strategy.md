# Stop Naming Your Resume "resume.pdf" — Do This Instead

**Topic/Category:** Career Advice / Job Search Strategy
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** Teach entry-level candidates and students a simple but impactful trick for naming their resume file to stand out in a stack of applications.

---

## Executive Summary

This reel (day 22 of a 300-day series on landing internships and full-time jobs) tackles a commonly overlooked detail: the filename of your resume PDF. The creator argues that submitting a file called "resume.pdf" makes you invisible in a recruiter's downloads folder. Instead, candidates should use a strategic naming convention — first name, last name, plus a differentiator like school, graduation year, target role, or key skills — to make their file instantly recognizable, searchable, and intentional.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Insight 1:** Naming your resume file "resume.pdf" is a missed branding opportunity — you become one anonymous file in a stack of thousands with the same name.
- **Insight 2:** Drop the word "resume" entirely from the filename. It's obvious from context that the file is a resume; spelling it out adds no value.
- **Insight 3:** Use the format `firstname_lastname_differentiator` where the differentiator can be your school + grad year, target role, or standout technical skills.
- **Insight 4:** Recruiters have responded positively to descriptive filenames — it signals intentionality and professionalism before they even open the document.
