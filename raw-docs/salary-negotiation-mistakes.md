# Salary Negotiation 101: Mistakes to Avoid

**Topic/Category:** Career Development / Job Search
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To provide quick, actionable advice on what NOT to do during a salary negotiation to maintain leverage and maximize the total compensation package.

---

## Executive Summary

This reel outlines four critical mistakes candidates make during salary negotiations. The core message is about maintaining leverage by letting the employer name the first number, grounding negotiations in real market data rather than lies, and looking beyond just the base salary to include the entire benefits package.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Never Speak the First Number:** The moment you provide a salary expectation, you lose leverage and potentially grant the employer a "discount."
- **Market Research is Essential:** Negotiation requires data. This includes online averages and, more importantly, real offers from competing companies for similar roles.
- **Honesty is the Best Policy:** Lying about other offers is easily detected and destroys your credibility and the employer's desire to hire you.
- **Negotiate the Whole Package:** Salary is just one component. You should also negotiate for PTO, sign-on bonuses, job titles, and other benefits.

- **Beginning (The Hook):** "Things you cannot do in a salary negotiation in under a minute."
- **Mistake #1: Giving a Number First:** The creator explains the "first rule of negotiation": he who speaks first loses. You must wait for their offer.
- **Mistake #2: Negotiating Without Research:** Breakdown of the two types of research: online averages and actual competing offers (the latter being more effective).
- **Mistake #3: Lying About Other Offers:** Warning against bluffing, as it leads to loss of leverage and potentially the job offer itself.
- **Mistake #4: Focusing Only on Base Salary:** Encouragement to look at the entire compensation package.
