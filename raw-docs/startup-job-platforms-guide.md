# 7 Best Platforms for Startup Internships & Jobs

**Topic/Category:** Career Development / Job Search
**Primary Goal of the Post:** To provide a curated list of effective platforms for finding internships and jobs at funded startups, moving beyond generic job boards like Naukri or Indeed.

---

## Executive Summary

This carousel highlights that startups rarely post on traditional platforms like Naukri. Instead, they use specialized job boards where candidates can often interact directly with founders or decision-makers. The post provides a breakdown of seven such platforms, including specific tips for maximizing visibility on each, such as profile optimization and targeted filtering.

## Key Insights & Main Ideas

- **Traditional Platforms are Ineffective for Startups:** Generic job boards (Naukri, Indeed) are not where most funded startups look for talent.
- **Direct Access to Founders:** Platforms like Hirect and Work at a Startup allow for direct communication with founders or small hiring teams, bypassing traditional HR filters.
- **Profile Matching > Application Count:** Modern startup job boards (Cutshort, Instahyre) use AI matching algorithms that reward high-quality, complete profiles over mass applying.
- **Strategic Filtering on LinkedIn:** LinkedIn can still be effective if filtered specifically for small-to-medium startups (11-200 employees) and acted upon quickly (within 24-48 hours).

## Detailed Breakdown / Step-by-Step

- **Slide 1: Hook:** Startups don't post on Naukri. Here is where they actually hire.
- **Slide 2: Wellfound (wellfound.com):** Formerly AngelList Talent. Largest dedicated startup board globally. Used by companies like Stripe, Notion, Figma. Tip: Set profile to "open to opportunities" for inbound interest.
- **Slide 3: Work at a Startup (workatastartup.com):** Run by Y Combinator. Only YC-backed companies post here. Tip: Filter by the latest YC batch for the most active hiring.
- **Slide 4: Cutshort (cutshort.io):** India-focused AI matching. Used by Sequoia/Accel-backed companies. Tip: Complete 100% of your profile to avoid being deprioritized by the algorithm.
- **Slide 5: Hirect (hirect.in):** Direct chat with founders/CEOs. No recruiters. Tip: Set precise roles and skills; response time is typically 24-48 hours.
- **Slide 6: HasJob (hasjob.co):** Curated Indian tech job board. Small, clean, and manually reviewed. Tip: Check weekly due to the high quality-to-volume ratio.
- **Slide 7: Instahyre (instahyre.com):** AI-powered hiring for funded Indian startups (Series A to C). Tip: Fill profile completely and toggle "open to work" for recruiters to browse your profile.
- **Slide 8: LinkedIn (linkedin.com):** Use with specific filters. 
    - **Step-by-step:** Jobs -> Search role + "intern" -> All filters -> Company size: 11-200 -> Date posted: past week -> Sort: most recent -> Apply before 48 hrs.
- **Slide 9: Summary:** List of all 7 platforms. Call to action to "save this" and "open one new platform today."
