# The "Third Door" Networking Strategy & Elite Cold Email Template

**Topic/Category:** Career Development / Networking
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To share a proven cold email template and networking philosophy ("The Third Door") for landing interviews and opportunities.

---

## Executive Summary

The video introduces a networking philosophy from the book *The Third Door* by Alex Banayan, which posits that there is always a "third door" (a non-traditional path) to success, whether it's finding a job, internship, or research opportunity. The creator shares the specific cold email template used by Banayan to land interviews with high-profile figures like Bill Gates and Lady Gaga, emphasizing brevity, credibility, and low friction for the recipient.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **The "Third Door" Philosophy:** Success often requires finding an unconventional entry point—metaphorically "climbing over a fence" or "jumping through a window"—rather than waiting in the line at the "front door" or buying your way into the "second door."
- **Brevity is Key:** Cold emails should be extremely concise (taking only 60 seconds to read) to respect the recipient's time.
- **Establish Credibility:** Briefly state who you are and why you should be taken seriously to build trust quickly.
- **Specific Ask:** Clearly define what you want from the recipient with a "very specific question."
- **Low-Friction Closing:** Acknowledge the recipient's busy schedule and ask for a minimal response (one or two lines) to increase the likelihood of a reply.

### Elite Cold Email Template
> Dear So-and-So,
>
> I know you're really busy and that you get a lot of emails, so this will only take sixty seconds to read.
> [Here is where you say who you are: add one or two lines that establish your credibility.]
> [Here is where you ask your very specific question.]
> I totally understand if you're too busy to respond, but even a one- or two-line reply would really make my day.
