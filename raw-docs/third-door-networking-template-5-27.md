# The "Third Door" Networking Strategy: The Elite Cold Email Template

**Topic/Category:** Networking / Career Advice
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To share an effective cold email template for networking, based on the concept of "The Third Door" by Alex Banayan.

---

## Executive Summary

The creator shares a powerful networking strategy inspired by Alex Banayan's book, *The Third Door*. The core idea is that there is always a "third door"—an unconventional path to reach high-profile people or secure opportunities when standard methods fail. The video highlights a specific cold email template used by Banayan to land interviews with icons like Bill Gates and Lady Gaga, emphasizing extreme brevity, quick credibility, and a low-pressure request for a response.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **The Philosophy of the Third Door:** In any pursuit—whether job hunting, internships, or research—there's a hypothetical "third door" that involves unconventional effort and persistence (e.g., jumping through windows) when the "front door" is crowded and the "VIP door" is exclusive.
- **Brevity as a Sign of Respect:** High-profile individuals value their time; explicitly stating that an email takes only 60 seconds to read reduces the mental friction for the recipient.
- **Immediate Credibility:** Use a maximum of two lines to establish why you are worth listening to.
- **Specificity is Crucial:** A vague ask is easy to ignore. The template requires a "very specific question."
- **Lower the Barrier to Respond:** Explicitly stating that a "one- or two-line reply" is sufficient makes it much easier for a busy person to say yes.

## Verbatim Templates & Scripts

> Dear So-and-So,
> 
> I know you're really busy and that you get a lot of emails, so this will only take sixty seconds to read.
> [Here is where you say who you are: add one or two lines that establish your credibility.]
> [Here is where you ask your very specific question.]
> I totally understand if you're too busy to respond, but even a one- or two-line reply would really make my day.
> 
> All the best,
> Tim
