# The Third Door Cold Email Strategy

**Topic/Category:** Career Networking & Outreach
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To share an "elite" cold email template based on the "Third Door" concept for networking with high-profile individuals and recruiters.

---

## Executive Summary

The video introduces the "Third Door" philosophy—a concept from Alex Banayan's book where networking involves finding unconventional ways ("the third door") to reach people. The creator presents a specific, concise cold email template designed to respect the recipient's time while establishing credibility and asking a focused question, claiming it's the same method used to interview figures like Bill Gates and Lady Gaga.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **The Third Door Concept:** There are always three ways in: the main entrance (the line), the VIP entrance (the privileged), and the third door (the window/fence/unconventional path).
- **Conciseness is Key:** The email template explicitly states it will only take 60 seconds to read, respecting the recipient's busy schedule.
- **Low Friction Requests:** Instead of a heavy ask, the template suggests asking for a "one- or two-line reply," making it much easier for a busy person to respond.
- **Credibility First:** Successful cold emails require a brief (1-2 line) establishment of who you are and why you're credible.

## Verbatim Templates & Scripts

### The "Third Door" Cold Email Template

> Dear So-and-So,
>
> I know you're really busy and that you get a lot of emails, so this will only take sixty seconds to read.
>
> [Here is where you say who you are: add one or two lines that establish your credibility.]
>
> [Here is where you ask your very specific question.]
>
> I totally understand if you're too busy to respond, but even a one- or two-line reply would really make my day.
>
> All the best,
> Tim
