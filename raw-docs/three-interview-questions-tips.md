# 3 Essential Questions to Ace Your Interview

**Topic/Category:** Career Development / Interview Preparation
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To provide viewers with three high-impact questions to ask at the end of an interview to build rapport and demonstrate critical thinking.

---

## Executive Summary

The video highlights that the questions asked at the end of an interview are crucial and can "make or break" the outcome. Drawing from personal success in securing multiple internships and six-figure job offers, the creator recommends three specific questions designed to engage the interviewer personally, show genuine interest, and demonstrate critical thinking. The goal is to leave a lasting, positive impression by establishing a personal connection.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Insight 1:** Asking about the interviewer's favorite projects builds rapport and shows genuine interest in their daily work.
- **Insight 2:** Asking what the interviewer would change about the company demonstrates critical thinking and authenticity, showing you're not just "saying the right thing."
- **Insight 3:** Asking why the interviewer stays at the company ends the interview on a positive, personal note, creating a memorable emotional connection.
- **Insight 4:** Successful interviews often involve building a personal connection that gets the interviewer laughing or sharing personal stories.

## Narrative & Step-by-Step Breakdown

- **Beginning:** The creator hooks the audience by stating the importance of post-interview questions and establishing their credibility as someone who has received top-tier offers.
- **Middle:** 
    1. **Question 1:** Focused on the interviewer's work ("What's a project you've really enjoyed...").
    2. **Question 2:** Focused on critical evaluation ("What's one thing you'd change...").
    3. **Question 3:** Focused on the interviewer's personal experience and loyalty ("What's made you stay...").
- **End:** The creator explains the psychological benefit of ending on a positive note and how to recognize when you've "locked" the offer through personal rapport.

## Verbatim Templates & Scripts

> **Interview Question 1:** "What's a project you've really enjoyed working on recently or something you're excited about in the next few months?"

> **Interview Question 2:** "What's one thing you'd change about the company or the team if you could?"

> **Interview Question 3:** "What's made you stay at the company as long as you have?"
