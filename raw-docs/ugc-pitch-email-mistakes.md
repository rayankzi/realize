## Key Insights & Main Ideas

- **Weak subject lines are the #1 open-rate killer:** Generic lines like "UGC Collab" or "Work together" look like spam and generate zero curiosity — brands have no reason to click.
- **Personalization beats volume:** Sending the same email to 100 brands is immediately detectable. A few varied templates, each customized with brand-specific details, perform far better.
- **You're reaching the wrong inbox:** Pitching to info@ or support@ means your email never reaches the marketing team. DM the brand on Instagram first to get the right contact.
- **Big brands = buried pitches:** Popular brands are flooded with creator emails. Small to mid-size brands are far more accessible and responsive.
- **Track your emails:** Using a tool like Mailsuite lets you see whether brands are opening your emails, so you know whether your subject line is working or if the issue is elsewhere.
