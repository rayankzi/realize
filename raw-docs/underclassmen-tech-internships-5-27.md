# The Ultimate List of Tech Internships & Fellowships for Freshmen & Sophomores

**Topic/Category:** Tech Careers / Internships
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To provide a comprehensive list of early-career internships and fellowships specifically designed for college freshmen and sophomores in the technology sector.

---

## Executive Summary

Ella Gonzales, a former Microsoft intern, provides a high-value roadmap for underclassmen looking to break into the tech industry. Drawing from her own success with the Microsoft Explore program—which she claims "literally set me up for life"—she presents a curated list of over 30 opportunities. The reel balances specific program recommendations with broader career advice, highlighting the importance of mentorship-focused fellowships and staying updated on shifting program statuses.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Early Career Catalyst:** Participating in underclassmen-specific programs (freshman/sophomore year) provides a massive head start and can define a student's career trajectory.
- **Internships vs. Fellowships:** While both are valuable, fellowships often place a heavier emphasis on mentorship, training, and open-source contributions, and are frequently shorter in duration.
- **Dynamic Landscape:** Program names and availability change frequently. For example, Google STEP has evolved into the Associate Software Developer Intern (ASDI) role, while Meta University has recently closed.
- **Key Recommendation:** Microsoft Explore is highly recommended and remains open and accepting freshmen for the current cycle.
- **Centralized Resources:** GitHub repositories are excellent tools for tracking a wide array of student opportunities in one place.
