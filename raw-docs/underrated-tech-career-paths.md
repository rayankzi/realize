# Underrated Tech Careers Your School Never Told You About

**Topic/Category:** Tech Career Advice / Career Development
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To introduce students and job seekers to less-talked-about tech career paths (DevRel, Technical Writing, AI Training, UX Research) that offer high pay or lower barriers to entry compared to traditional software engineering.

## Executive Summary

The video highlights four specific tech career paths that are often overlooked in academic settings: Developer Relations (DevRel), Technical Writing, AI Training, and UX Research. The creator emphasizes that these roles often prioritize communication, psychology, or specific evaluative skills over pure coding, making them excellent alternatives for those in or adjacent to the computer science field who may want different types of challenges or lower entry barriers.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **DevRel (Developer Relations):** Acts as a bridge between a company and its developer community. It requires a mix of technical knowledge and strong communication skills.
- **Technical Writing:** A high-demand field because clear software documentation is rare. Companies are willing to pay a premium for people who can "translate" technical concepts for users.
- **AI Training:** A burgeoning entry-level field that involves rating and correcting AI outputs to improve models. Notably, it often doesn't require a CS degree.
- **UX Research:** Focuses on the "why" behind user behavior through interviews and psychology. It’s a tech-adjacent role that is often filled by people with psychology or communication backgrounds.
