# 10 Ways to Make Your Vibe-Coded App Look Professional

**Topic/Category:** Software Development / AI Tools / UI/UX Design
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To provide actionable tips for developers using "vibe coding" (prompt-based coding) to ensure their applications look high-quality, unique, and professional rather than generic or rushed.

---

## Executive Summary

The video addresses the common issue where AI-generated (vibe-coded) apps look strikingly similar due to limited prompt libraries and common templates. The creator shares a list of strategies to break this mold, emphasizing the use of specific prompt libraries, thorough product requirements, visual replication of existing high-end designs, and the use of "skills files" to achieve professional-grade results.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Avoid Generic Looks:** Vibe-coded apps often suffer from a "samey" aesthetic because many creators use the same base prompt libraries.
- **Strategic PRDs:** A Product Requirement Document (PRD) is essential for clarity and defining the "moat" of the application (e.g., vertical AI, custom vector databases).
- **Visual Replication:** Modern AI models are exceptionally good at replicating the design and layout of a website from a simple screenshot.
- **The Power of Skills Files:** "Skills files" are essentially high-quality, specialized markdown prompts that can guide AI to replicate complex designs (like Stripe or Linear) with high fidelity.
- **Community over Tools:** Building professional apps requires both the right technical approach and a community for feedback and growth.

## Verbatim Templates & Scripts

> **PRD Principles to Remember**
> - A good PRD focuses on the WHAT, not the HOW
> - Requirements should be specific, measurable, and testable
> - Each feature should trace back to a real user need
> - Clear prioritization helps prevent scope creep
> - PRDs evolve over time as you learn more about your users

> **Technical Considerations**
