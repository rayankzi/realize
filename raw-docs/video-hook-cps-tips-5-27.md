# Boost Your Video Views with CPS: The Cuts Per Second Secret

**Topic/Category:** Social Media Marketing / Content Creation Strategy
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To teach content creators how to increase their video views by increasing the frequency of visual cuts (CPS) in the video hook.

---

## Executive Summary

This reel uses an interactive "Video A vs. Video B" guessing game to reveal a core retention tactic: **CPS (Cuts Per Second)**. The creator demonstrates that high-performing videos (millions of views) typically feature 3 to 6 cuts within the hook, while lower-performing videos often rely on a single, static shot. The main takeaway is that increasing visual variety early in the video is a simple but powerful way to grab and hold viewer attention.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **The CPS Rule:** CPS stands for "Cuts Per Second." It is a metric used to gauge the visual pace of a video.
- **The Magic Number:** High-performing videos often have **3 to 6 cuts/shots** in the first few seconds (the hook).
- **Static Hooks Fail:** Videos with only **one shot** in the hook tend to have lower engagement and fewer views because they fail to visually stimulate the viewer quickly enough.
- **Visual Attention:** Adding more shots or cuts is a low-effort, high-impact way to make a video hook more visually engaging.
