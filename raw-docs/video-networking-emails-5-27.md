# Stop Sending Boring Networking Emails: Use Video to Stand Out

**Topic/Category:** Career Advice / Job Searching / Networking
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To encourage job seekers, especially new grads, to use video messaging tools like Vidyard or Loom to stand out to recruiters and hiring managers.

---

## Executive Summary

This reel addresses the common frustration of being ignored when networking for jobs or internships. The creator argues that traditional text-based emails are overused and boring. Instead, he suggests using video messaging tools to create a personalized, visual impact that immediately grabs a recruiter's attention. By embedding a video directly into an email, candidates can showcase their personality and initiative, significantly increasing their chances of getting a response.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Personalization via Video:** Sending a video message (using tools like Vidyard or Loom) is the "most underrated way" to contact recruiters.
- **Visual Impact:** A recruiter seeing your face in their inbox is far more engaging than another wall of text.
- **Differentiate Your Approach:** The key to successful networking is doing what others aren't. If everyone is sending boring emails, you must do something different to stand out.
- **Tool Recommendation:** Use Vidyard or Loom to record and embed videos that appear as clickable thumbnails in an email.
