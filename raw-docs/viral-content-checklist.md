# The 6-Step Viral Content Checklist for 100,000 Views

**Topic/Category:** Social Media Strategy / Content Creation
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To provide a specific, actionable checklist of elements that contribute to a video's virality and high view counts.

---

## Executive Summary

This content outlines a repeatable framework for achieving high view counts on social media videos. It emphasizes a structured approach consisting of six key elements: a "High-concept" idea, two types of hooks (curiosity and "super"), immediate value delivery, unique ("net new") insights, and a script designed to maintain tension. The message is that virality is a result of following a disciplined checklist rather than random luck.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Strategic Ideation:** Starting with a "High-tam" (likely high-concept or high-intent) idea that has broad appeal.
- **Hook Layering:** Utilizing both a "curiosity hook" to pique interest and a "super hook" to ensure the viewer stays.
- **Value Delivery Efficiency:** "Speed to value" is critical; getting to the point quickly prevents viewer drop-off.
- **Originality:** Providing "net new value"—information or perspectives that are not already saturated in the market.
- **Structured Retention:** Using a "tension script" to keep the audience engaged through a narrative or informational arc.
