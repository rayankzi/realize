## Key Insights & Main Ideas

- **Hooks are the bottleneck:** Most creators lose viewers in the first 1–3 seconds due to a weak or generic opening line, not poor editing or content quality.
- **There are five distinct hook archetypes:** Success hooks, Problem-Solving hooks, Storytelling hooks, Intriguing Question hooks, and Curiosity hooks — each designed to trigger a different psychological pull.
- **Templates use brackets as variables:** Every hook is a fill-in-the-blank formula (e.g., `[challenge]`, `[goal]`, `[time frame]`), making them immediately reusable across any niche.
- **The cover itself models the lesson:** Using "me at 10" with a childhood photo is a live example of a storytelling/success hook — the creator is teaching by doing.
- **Hooks work across all content types:** The frameworks apply to lifestyle, business, education, finance, fitness, and any other niche with minimal modification.

## Verbatim Templates & Scripts

### Success Hooks

> - I turned [challenge] into [success], and you can too.
> - Want to know how I achieved [goal]? Here's my secret.
> - If I can go from [struggle] to [goal], you're next.
> - I made [number] in [time frame] by doing [simple action].
> - Here's how I went from [low point] to [high point].
> - My journey from "meh" to "heck yeah" in [short time frame].
> - I did [amazing result] in [time frame] — you can too, trust me.

### Problem-Solving Hooks

> - Struggling with [common problem]? Here's the solution you've been looking for.
> - Tired of [problem]? Try this simple fix.
> - I used to [describe problem], until I found this [solution].
> - If you're dealing with [issue], this is for you.
> - Don't know how to [achieve goal]? Let me show you how.
> - What if I told you solving [problem] is easier than you think?
> - Say goodbye to [problem] with this simple trick.

### StoryTelling Hooks

> - What no one tells you about starting out in [topic].
> - The one thing I stopped doing that made all the difference in my [business/life].
> - I used to struggle with staying consistent, but then I found this simple trick.
> - What I learned the hard way, so you don't have to.
> - What I learned from my biggest failure, and how it changed my approach to business.
> - I still remember the day when everything changed.
> - You won't believe what happened next after I decided to [action].

### Intriguing Question Hooks

> - Do you want to [achieve goal] without [common obstacle]?
> - What's stopping you from [achieving goal]?
> - Have you ever wondered why [unexpected fact]?
> - Would you try [method] if it could [desired result]?
> - What would you do if you knew you couldn't fail?
> - Do you know the secret to [achieving a goal] faster?
> - Have you tried [this thing] yet? If not, you're missing out.

### Curiosity Hooks

> - What if I told you that you've been doing [common task] wrong this whole time?
> - Ever wondered why [common belief] doesn't work?
> - You'll never believe what happened when I tried [unexpected action].
> - Do you know the #1 mistake [your audience] make when [doing something]?
> - Here's what no one tells you about [topic].
> - Why is everyone suddenly obsessed with [unexpected trend]?
> - Here's why [unexpected action] might be the smartest move you make.
