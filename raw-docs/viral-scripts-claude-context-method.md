# Writing Viral Content Scripts with Claude AI: The Context Method

**Topic/Category:** Content Creation / AI Tools
**Primary Goal of the Post:** Teach content creators how to use Claude AI effectively — not as a ghostwriter, but as a thinking partner — by giving it the right context to produce content that sounds authentic and personal.

---

## Executive Summary

Most people who use Claude for content creation get robotic-sounding output because they simply ask it to "write" without giving it any context. This carousel by @arushiiishokeen reframes Claude as a "thinking partner" rather than a ghostwriter: by front-loading your audience profile, tone examples, and the emotional outcome you want, you can generate a full week of on-brand content in just 3 prompts and 10 minutes.

## Key Insights & Main Ideas

- **The common mistake:** People ask Claude to write without providing context, resulting in generic, robotic content that sounds like it was written "at 2am."
- **The reframe:** Claude is a thinking partner, not a ghostwriter — give it your context and it mirrors your voice back at you.
- **Three inputs matter most:** (1) who your audience is, (2) your tone with real examples (not vague descriptors like "friendly & professional"), and (3) the specific feeling you want your audience to leave with.
- **The payoff:** 3 prompts + 10 minutes = a full week of content that actually sounds like you.
