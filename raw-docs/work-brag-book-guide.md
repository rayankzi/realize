# How to Create a Brag Book for Career Growth

**Topic/Category:** Career Development / Workplace Success
**Primary Goal of the Reel:** To teach employees how to document their professional achievements (a "brag book") to make performance reviews, raise requests, and job searches easier.

---

## Executive Summary

The video emphasizes the importance of documenting work achievements in a "brag book" or "task tracker." By tracking specific contributions, screenshots of praise, and professional development weekly, employees can build a body of evidence that proves their value. This practice simplifies high-stakes conversations like performance reviews and job interviews by providing concrete data on how their work supports broader company goals.

## Core Insights & Takeaways

- **Document Everything:** Professional success isn't just about doing the work; it's about being able to prove the work you did.
- **Minimum Tracking Requirements:** At a minimum, track the Date, the Task/Project, and your specific Contribution.
- **Quantify Impact:** Connect your work to company-level goals like revenue, customer satisfaction, and efficiency.
- **Store Proof:** Save screenshots of praise and feedback immediately so they aren't lost or forgotten.
- **Consistency is Key:** Adding just one item per week ensures the information stays fresh and the task doesn't become overwhelming.

## Narrative & Step-by-Step Breakdown

- **Beginning:** Introduction to the concept of documenting work ("brag book" or "task tracker") as a necessity for career advancement.
- **Middle:** 
    - **Tools:** Suggests using Google Sheets, Excel, or Notion.
    - **Structure:** Recommends specific columns (Date, Project Description, Your Contribution, Stakeholders, Contribution to Company Goals, and Notes).
    - **Supporting Evidence:** Encourages adding sections for praise (screenshots), courses, skills, and training.
- **End:** A call to action to update the document weekly to prepare for future resumes, raises, or performance reviews.

## Verbatim Templates & Scripts

### Brag Book Column Template
> 1. **Date:** When it happened (so you have a timeline of impact).
> 2. **Project Description:** What the project or task actually was.
> 3. **Your Contribution:** What you did. Not the team. YOU.
> 4. **Stakeholders:** Who was involved or benefited?
> 5. **Contribution to Company Goals:** How it supported revenue, efficiency, customer satisfaction, team success, etc.
> 6. **Notes:** This could be feedback, results, data, or anything you want to remember later.

## Hook & Call to Action

- **The Hook:** "You need to learn how to document everything at work. And the first step is documenting the work that you do."
- **Call to Action:** "Start doing this now and I promise your future self will thank you." (Caption also suggests doing it weekly).
